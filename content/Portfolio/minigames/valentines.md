# Valentines
*(During 14th of February)*

## ⚙️ Commands ⚙️

- 💌 `/valentines send` - Send someone a valentine.
- 💝 `/valentines valentine` - Send  someone a secret valentine.
- 💓 `/valentines lovemeter` - Check your compatibility with someone.
### Cupid

Use 🏹 `/valentines heart` commands to affect someone's heart!
- 💘 `/valentines heart shoot` - Break someone's heart.
- 💔 `/valentines heart mend` - Mend someone's broken heart if your's broken too.
- 💞 `/valentines heart info` - Check state of someone's heart.
- 🛡 `/valentines heart protect` - Protect someone's heart from changing.

### Matchmaker

Use 💌 `/valentines matchmaker` commands to partake in matchmaking process.
- 💗 `/valentines matchmaker check` - Check your matches!
- 💖 `/valentines matchmaker profile` - Set up your profile for matchmaking.
- 💕 `/valentines matchmaker search` - Find your matches by browse profiles of others.
