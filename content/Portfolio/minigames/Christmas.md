*(December)*
## Gifts
- Collect 🎁 appearing under messages.
- Gift `Gold Present` to another user using `/christmas gift` (For this, you need to have 🎁 to send!)
- Grinch your way through to receive `Rotten Present` when using `/christmas steal` (For this, the user you are trying to steal from has to have 🎁 to steal!)
- You can view the leaderboard for collected 🎁, gifted `Gold Presents` and stolen `Rotten Presents` presents with `/leaderboard event` command.

## Snowballs
- Gather snow ❄️ using `/snowball gather` to get throwable snowballs or collect ❄️ appearing under messages.
- Throw snowball at another user using `/snowball throw`.
- You can view leaderboard for thrown, missed and hit snowballs with `/leaderboard event` command.

## Miscellaneous
- Collect daily advent thingi using `/christmas advent`. There's a leaderboard for that as well
- Show another person your appreciation and send them a cookie! `/christmas cookie`. Leaderboard is only to view received cookies, otherwise it's meaningless.
- Put Christmas Hat on your avatar with `/christmas hat`! No one is responsible if the hat ends up covering your entire personal-digital-pixel-space.
