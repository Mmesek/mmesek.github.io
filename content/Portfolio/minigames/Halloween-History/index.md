# 2021
**2nd edition**
*As the Night falls and the moon turns crimson, 
As the monsters come out of hiding...
It's scary to be on the streets on your own*

*As the lust for blood arises and the fog settles,
As the creatures of the night bite more fiercely...
The streets are becoming stained in crimson*

A *Mysterious Potion* has appeared in your local dealer's offer, some say it grants a person who drinks an immunity, others however report side effects that include - but are not limited to - photophobia, decreased UV protection, spontaneous howling at the moon and compulsive biting living from no reason.

In response, numerous *Guilds* have reopened their recruitment offices to refill their ranks and fight this darkness once again.

Will you answer the call of the dark and `drink` the potion, or will you `enlist` and fight?
The Choice is yours, but be firm and make your decision before it's made for you...

*You decide to take a peek outside, where you see bodies lying on the ground dried from any blood whatsoever.*

*You make haste for the bookshelf looking for a shelf labelled "Darkest Hour". Once you have finally located it, you find two items: a vial with liquid, and a book...*

What will you do, will you forsake your humanity and join the condemned, or would you rather fight to keep your humanity till the very end? 

As always, the Choice is yours, but beware - you should be swift with your decision, or else the choice won't be yours anymore...
