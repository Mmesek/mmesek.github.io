## Trick or treat?
*(October)*

There are `Treats` 🍬 you can collect, and who knows, perhaps you'll need them later on? There's enough of them for everyone, so anyone who clicks within a short while after they appear, will get some!

Once monsters ☠️ come knocking, they'll react with scary 🕸️ reactions 🦴...  You don't want them to catch you unprepared! If you, however, have enough `Treats` 🍬 for them, be the first to click and they'll reward you with `Fear` 🦴! Otherwise, nothing but a trick is awaiting you and they'll take some of `Fear` 🦴 you already have away as a trick!

### Commands
- 🦴 `/leaderboard event Fear` to check who has most fear.  
- 🍬 `/leaderboard event Candies` to check how much candies you have.

# Mini-games
## Biting
*(14th of October to 7th of November)*

### Commands
- `/halloween humans` allows you to `drink` the liquid to become a monster, or `enlist` and become a hunter
- `/halloween monster` allows you to `bite` another user and turn them into monster of your kind
- `/halloween hunter` allows you to `cure` monster you are hunting and turn them back into Human, as well as `defend` to protect fellow Hunter from being turned into another monster, `betray` has a thin chance to convince a monster you are hunting to join hunters

**Cheat-sheet of bites/cures**:
- `Vampire Hunters` can only cure `Vampires`
- `Huntsmen` can only cure `Werewolves`
- `Enchanters` can only cure `Zombies`.
- Monsters (`Vampires`, `Werewolves` and `Zombies`) can bite anyone that isn't hunting them or isn't a fellow monster
- You can only change faction when you are human or when you are bitten or cured by another 

### **Statistics**:
- `/halloween leaderboard` Top cures/bites
- `/halloween statistics` Factions numbers
- `/halloween info` Cooldowns, faction & protection status
- `/halloween history` Turn history
## Fear
*(14th of October to 7th of November)*

Wondering what you can do with 🦴 `Fear` you've collected so far?

Wonder no more and `Summon` an army! Use them to `Scare` your opposition out of their 🦴`Fear` or `Raid` bosses to gain even more 🦴`Fear`, and once you get bored with your old `Imps`.... just `Sacrifice` them! You'll 👻`Reinforce` your 🦴`Fear` this way and land yourself a spot on a leader-board!

- `/halloween fear` - to access mini-game's related commands
- `/leaderboard event Reinforced Fear` - To check leader-board of this mini-game

**Few things to note**:
- You **can not** scare people if their army is too powerful or too weak (same applies to bosses). Looking for a fair fight? Investigate possible target armies with `/halloween fear scout`
- You can sacrifice your hard earned 🎃 Pumpkins by carving them with `/halloween fear carve`, this will increase your protection against being scared, however it'll also affect your position on leader-board with 🎃 pumpkins so choose wisely.
- This is unrelated to monster/hunter mini-game.
