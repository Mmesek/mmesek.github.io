# Wakey Wakey
Woke up something around 9am, and then took a walk around a nearby lake with my host towards the train station. There, got a croissant with marzipan from Le Crobag (freaking good one too! Or I was simply too hungry to tell) for breakfast and... 4h after waking up, and something like 2h after arriving at the station, finally boarded train to Central Station slightly before 13.

![Lake nearby|800x600](lake.jpg)

# Hamburg Card

Wait what. 4h and I've done nothing but walk hungry in circles?
Well... I was... researching if gourmet upgrade to Hamburg Card is worth. 
It is, if you: 
1. Plan on eating
2. Have people in your group

Neither of these was the case for me however. 

After finally making up my mind, I've went through process of purchasing Hamburg Card. Not once, but twice, and that includes payment authorization ;) No, I wasn't buying for someone else, system refused to even sell me said card first time around.

I mean... The card is worth it if you are willing to spend money...
(In general, trips alone covered pass costs and saved me worry to buy them, even though I didn't meet any controllers, the extra discount was nice as well.)

With Hamburg Card, which includes public transportation, on the train, I was checking which train I'm supposed to switch to on HVV App. 
As the train was late, App displayed different route which... Required me to go the other direction. Yay, more train hopping!
Finally arrived... Somewhere near Rathaus (city hall, nothing to do with rats). 

![Rathaus building](rathaus.jpg)

From there I've wandered aimlessly trying to find something to eat, browsed bunch of places, though, not much was nearby. Finally found one online and went there... only to stop halfway through at a bakery on the way. Achievement unlocked, failed to eat at the destination.

So instead of proper meal I've opted for a ciabatta sandwich. Eh, happens.
# Chocoversum

Afterwards went to Chocoversum to make my own chocolate. English guide was in half an hour, so I've asked nicely if I could recharge my phone using the plug nearby, and managed to squeeze a whole lot of additional 10% in 20 minutes that I've wasted trying to get city card... Eh, at least we got it back.

![Machine to mix Chocolate](chocoversum.jpg)

First chocolate tasting was molten chocolate over a waffle. I may or may not have gotten a *little* too greedy and slightly spilled that over myself. Ugh, got a tissue so could fix it up before it did any serious damage... Wait, why is my phone covered in chocolate? Wh... There's somehow chocolate in pocket I usually keep my phone. Okay, don't panic, it's not that bad... Okay, actually panic, it's bad. Is there a bathroom? No, we'll last until end of it, it's not *that* bad. Wait, what was first room about? Origins of Chocolate? Some raw coco bean?

Guide was nice, though quite a bit was dedicated to Fair Trade education and related labels. I guess they are pushing for sustainability? It still was quite lengthy. My previous visit to similar museum in Poznań few years ago didn't focus so heavily on that topic. Or perhaps it wasn't so widespread yet? 

Tasting phases were cool, though raw coco beans is... not my cup of chocolate for sure.
During workshop on making our own chocolate, tour guide told us that for brave ones there's chili and wasabi peas. Ok. Let's do this. White Chocolate with brave option and few other nice stuff that looked nice because why not! 

![White chocolate with a lot of wasabi peas](chocolate.jpg "When I came back home, I've finally tried it, sadly during remaining 3 weeks of travel it got seriously crashed :( Also... do not recommend wasabi peas. Does not taste of wasabi." )

We had few more tastings (6 in total) during various stages of chocolate production, and I've acted as a photograph for a bunch of different people during last stage near giant chocolate bar 😎

After leaving nearly 2h later, I... had 10% of battery less anyway. Okay, gotta clean that phone from chocolate now. Eh, gotta push armor case a little away to wipe some remaining chocolate out... Power menu notification? Hmm, It should be fine. Few seconds later... it's not fine, my phone is restarting. *Panic mode*. Ok let's just boot it up again. PIN? I know my PIN right? Phew, I do. Ok, where to next? Let's take a walk... And then... my battery started dropping LIKE CRAZY, like 10% in 10 minutes. What's going on? *Full panic mode*. Down to 35%, yikes.

# Battery Hunt

What's on the list? Miniature Wunderland nearby. Okay, maybe I could charge phone there like I did at Chocoversum. When I've found the place, I had to go to like 4th floor for entrance. Upon arrival, I went to the first person that seemed like they are working there. Asked my question and got answer that there are chargers in lockers upstairs after entering. Huh, alright... We do need a ticket though and I think I've entered through wrong gate. So I went back, entered again, went through empty queue-line and found myself in exact same place I've been before. Great. Absolutely great. Cash, yes, ticket. Hello, I would like to get a ticket for this place. And then I'm asked which timeslot interests me, so I look at the display below and... nearest slot is at 19 or so. It's 16. I'll... be back later.

Desperate, went on a hunt for a restaurant that would both feed me AND my phone. I pick first place nearby that I've pinned earlier from some online recommendations, and go there. It seems... pretty fancy, is this the right place? I did select budget options, right?

Hmm... Okay, anyway, I asked waiter if I could charge my phone, they led me inside. Ok, cool, not many people, still a fancy place though. They show me a table, and ask me for my phone. As I pass it, it's still connected to me by a cord I've made. Yeah, I do not like the idea of disconnecting it, but ok, sadly need that battery so a little fiddling to unchain it and there you go.

All good, phone's charging and they give me menu. It is a fancy place. Not in my budget range. Yikes. Anything nice or cheap? Nada, Darn. Ok, let's... Why this menu only has two pages?! Ok, let's jump onto map and check if this is for sure this place, and maybe find another close by. Aaaand waitress comes. Gotta make my escape, so I apologize saying it's not for my pocket as I thought it was a different place and I wasn't sure why I had a pin on this place. They look at my map and tell me that's the place. Yeeaaah... I'll better go.

5% more, Okay. Let's go to another one from the map. 
Hey, can I charge my pho... almost insta no. Ouch, let's go. I walked around for a bit, now actually hungry, then passed one Italian place. Okay, that could work. Let's check if there's something else around maybe... Nope, circling back we enter the La Sepia. Still looks fancy, where am I for the duck. Anyway, Fine if I charge my phone? Yes! They accepted my terms... Showed me in a general direction to the table, so I went and I was confused for a moment which table they meant, there was one with a socket for like 4 people and one without for 2... Right... Standing confused achieved nothing, so I sat at the one for 4 people and then... HEY! YOU ALONE?! Uhh... I was told to... Oh okay fine that's alright.

😭I just need some extra battery, I'm new around here. Ok ok, battery's charging, what's on the menu? Pasta, cool. With Salmon? Even cooler. That works for me. Nothing to drink? Uhh... not right now, thanks. And then they gave me simplest pasta out there ;( And this place looked decent! (and costed too).

# Miniature Wunderland

After walking around, doing some photos and videos took a bus to Miniature Wunderland. It was after 22 already and I could finally enter, and on top of that, there was a discount! I'm being asked where I'm from, and they tell me it's for statistics. So I tell them and receive brochure in my language! Cool.

I pass gift shop and enter, there's a statistics panel which tells how many people came from which country. There's also one 👽 alien with "Mars" displaying 0. Interesting, I wonder if someone tried to say at the ticket office that they are from Mars... ;)

After a while of contemplating miniatures... lights go off, and I'm like, did the sun die? Who killed the lights? Few moments later (maybe a minute), there's a communicate saying something in German, after similar scenario repeated few times I think they were saying that it's a feature and that people shouldn't panic. Later I've found some plate (or read in brochure) saying there's a day-night cycle.

![Miniature city|800x600](miniature_city.jpg)

There was also a communicate that they are closing cafeteria in few minutes. I'm a bit hungry so let's check what they have to offer. There, I thought there's a yoghurt dessert behind glass like the label was saying, so half-happy I took it (there wasn't really anything else left to pick anyway) and proceeded to cash, where the price is higher by 2€! Wait what? I thought dessert is... that's... not dessert? But it was behind glass... oh... that's salad? Uh ok. Turned out it was a Cuscus Salad (I didn't have cuscus before so...).

Afterwards I went to take a walk around the place, this time not *that* hungry, and after walking around probably entire place went back to host's place around midnight or so. However since I was half dead, I may have not messaged them and had to wait a while. We've chatted until 2am. Then I proceeded to repack for the next day and somehow went to sleep at roughly 4am. Yay.
