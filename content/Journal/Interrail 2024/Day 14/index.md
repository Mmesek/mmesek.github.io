3rd day in Barcelona starts with chatting at a hostel again, and almost checking out. Then decided to stay one more night as they offered quite a nice deal.

Then, I've met with friends that will join me for the rest of my trip. We went to get some breakfast at Lato Cafe where i've got Supreme Omelette.

There we've also found a good price for a hostel in Madrid that we booked. Afterwards, we went to Sagrada Familia, took some photos, walked around, and went to Gothic Quarter. 

![Shoes hanging from the cable](shoes.jpg)

There, decided to go to place where friends were staying to regroup before continuing. So we took a metro. When purchasing a ticket, a strange homeless guy in a mask appeared out of thin air, he was speaking multiple languages and told us to get family card as we can scan it multiple times and get 8 travels for 11.2€. After a bit of calculation we decided it's a perfect option - 3 tickets to the hotel, then 3 back to city, and 2 for friends to get back. 

![Empanadas with friends](empanadas.jpg)

After repacking we went to the train station, on way to which we had some empanadas with chorizo. We went to book seat reservations to Madrid, where we had to take a number and waited for something like half an hour for our turn.

![Crumbled cartoon castle](castle.jpg)

We took the metro to the beach, on which we've met 3 other travellers from Poland who were also going onto the beach, however since we had wrong destination, we've left too early and had to walk for a while. 

![Clouds behind a pillar](pillar.jpg)

While we were on our way, there was a guy controlling the traffic, and when he saw my arafatka, at first he shown us thumbs up, and then approached me. Showed to give it and tied it around my head. Afterwards, said that now I look like some sort of Prince and to make myself a photo in this style. Well... My friends told me it suits me, though I felt a little uneasy near security, especially after France. So I wanted to play it safe and reverted to my usual style after we went further away.

We've started searching for something to eat, settled upon nothing. So friends did groceries and I checked Too Good To Go nearby and there was a bakery 365. Decided to give it a shot for the first time and... Wow. That was actually a fine dinner for 4€. No photos because I was too hungry to make anything comprehensible, and there was quite a bit of bags involved too.

Walked back to the hostel, where I've chatted a little more with people, and while chilling at the rooftop, heard someone mentioned my next destinations. So I've joined the conversation, met fellow interrailers from Italy, participated in language exchange with Australians and Italians, and witnessed some serious joint being smoked.

One of my friends brought me lock from Poland. Too big for the locker though =(
