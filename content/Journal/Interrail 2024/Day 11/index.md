>The troubles pile up  
The road is known  
The danger lurks  
Yet I'm deadlocked

Off to the train station! Where I find out I can't purchase a reservation because there's no train from Gare de Nord to Lyon, and I'm supposed to go to Gare De Lyon. A bit of asking got me through the metro, though if I had known better, I would go there on foot 4km instead of paying 4€. In any case, that got me on a train to Lyon an hour earlier so it's not too bad after all as I got to be in Lyon a bit longer too while lollygagging at breakfast.

![Colored streets in Lyon](lyon_streets.jpg)

At Lyon, took a walk around. Went to a nearby restaurant and tried Salade Lyonaisse. Still prefer Caesar's sibling though.
When I went to pay, they charged me for a table next to mine and we then gave me cash in return after I mentioned it wasn't my order.
Bit tough considering im running cashless and going to Barcelona.

Then, managed to get onto the train, conductor told me to use next doors if I'm with interrail so I did. Then I found myself in first class for a while. 

A while until conductor came to check my ticket, however they didn't speak English so I had to use translator to make sure we are on the same page. Thankfully, we reached a consensus and I could pay with cash (I normally would use card like I was doing the whole time, but I kind of wanted to liquify extra cash from the restaurant). They also didn't charge extra so I paid 10€ for it.

![Fiesta, right after leaving train station in Barcelona](barcelona_fiesta.jpg)

After arriving, I went to one of the hostels I bookmarked. On spot, I've found out that I can only book online with credit card, as in person they required physical card and no cash whatsoever. Eh, alright. Then ai arrive in my room... And locker with my number is taken. Great.

So I go to reception to hopefully find some directions to a place to eat dinner. Paella on Thursdays for 12€? Let's try that.

So I arrive at the bar where they tell me it's on the terrace. Ok. So climb and I'm asked when did I buy it because... They make it at 8pm and it's not so good anymore, ffs. It's truly not good. There's also a free drink included so I look for something soft... Juice! And then I'm informed they are all out except orange. Ugh...

![Barcelona at night](barcelona_night.jpg)
