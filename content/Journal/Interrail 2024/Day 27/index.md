Half an hour for connection at Leipzig to Prague, thankfully, the train was open so Zi could board it as soon as possible and didn't need to wait in the freezing cold.

![](train.jpg)

Few hours later... Rise and shine! Heavy rain outside window. I feel like my compartment is the only warm place as I'm being thrown out of it by some family of 5 that pretends to be of 6. Light breakfast composed of marzipan crossaint as the rain outside seems to stop

![](prague_train_station_lego2.jpg)

Alright! Made it to Prague! Took a stroll around a train station, and then looked for a place to eat breakfast... 12min walk. And it's raining... Ugh... 

![](prague_building.jpg)

Alright, made it through. And I'm greeted with information that their card machine broke and they can only accept cash or euro, though according to menu they give -5% discount when paid with cash. Not ideal. I've went to the ATM machine nearby and was greeted with 4€ fee for withdrawal. Nope, thank you. 

![](prague_tower.jpg)

Moving on, I've found another place that sadly was more expensive but it food was alright. There, I've also realized two things:
- Revolut's currency conversion fee on weekend is no joke
- it's Saturday 
Because of second, I couldn't just transfer money from Wise because it wouldn't be instant, but also Revolut didn't want to provide me with CZK account details to do so.

![](rainy_streets.jpg)

![](park.jpg)

Eh, after dealing with unfavorable conditions, and rolling with them, found some Trdelník booth where I got a classic one. A waaay better than one I had in my hometown as it was far less sweet, though I thought it's called Kolac

![](trdelnik.jpg)

![](prague_church.jpg)
![](prague_sculpture.jpg)

![](tshirt_brick.jpg)

![Closed? Aww, Guess gotta revisit with a better weather!](brick_booth.jpg)

![](old_town.jpg)

![](sitting_figurine.jpg)

Still with few hours to pass before going for train to Vienna, I've looked for another cafe I had bookmarked: Café Svatého Václava. There, I was slightly disoriented by the prices (6€ for a juice?!), eventually settling on a potato soup after being driven out from any of my other considerations by the waiter who simply told me that they weren't Czech enough... And after asking for recommendations among cheaper options as I'm near the end of my journey, and so is my wallet.

![](soup.jpg)

In the end, I've paid in cash whatever I had left (sadly it was less than enough) and was going to pay remainder with card, as Revolut's fee would simply make it too expensive otherwise. However waiter after hearing I'm short few cents (roughly 6-7 CZK) decided it's fine and paid for. Thank you once again.

![](building.jpg)

![](cars_street.jpg)

![](prague_train_station.jpg)

After being back on the train station, I've grabbed mini koblížek as a dessert. Then, embarked the train to... Weather caught up with me once again, as both my train and train I would change to has been delayed. 

![](rain_drops.jpg)

Alternative connections? Rough. I've left at Stare Mosto as conductor told me it's my best shot, there, lost, started looking for connections, and found one to Breclav.

![](stare_mesto_train_station.jpg)

![](almost_2h_delay.jpg)

While looking for connection, I've met rather sketchy-at-first stranger with a skateboard, a ticket-less passenger with years of evading control experience who was also going to Vienna. Thanks for the cookies.


Well, if I would want to get to Vienna, I probably could try with him, however my plan changed as time of departure of night train grew closer - now my destination wasn't to get to Vienna but to catch the train back home and finish the trip. 


On the station of Breclav, I've managed to grab some mini pastries from shop for dinner. Guy from earlier told me about a shop nearby that was supposed to be open until 10pm, so we went there only to find out it was closed at 8pm. Oh well, so in the rain we came back.
![](sculpture.jpg)

![](alley_night.jpg)
After returning to the train station, I've met group of people also trying to get to Poland, meanwhile guy from earlier managed to get train to Vienna after all. Might've go as well but 5 degrees... Brrr....
