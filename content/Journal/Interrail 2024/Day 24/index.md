First things first: breakfast in a form of Cannala (that's what I learned egg sandwich is called) at Bar Central, at Central Mercado and an Hortatcha there. Then, walk back to grab my things and ready to set off to the train station, on the way, grabbed few crossaints (really need to lay them off) at the local bakery for later

![](sandwich.jpg)

![](valencia_street_day.jpg)

At the train station I think it was first time when they asked me to show my interrail pass in Spain while boarding. 

![What do you mean I was supposed to get Fideua in Valencia?](barcelona_fideua.jpg)

Traveling again, nearest layover: 2h in Barcelona where I settled on some Fideua at TapaTape near train station where I've been told wait time is 30m. Rough, but we still should make it. Food arrived after 20, and there still was some time so went to the bakery, got chocolate crossaint and... It was MEANT to be for later. Well, it didn't last that long as I ate it almost immediately. Debatable decision I know

While waiting for boarding I've met another interrailer also taking the same train. 
Somehow queue went smoothly while we were chatting as opposed to dead standing waiting to enter

![](barcelona_platform.jpg)

And finally, onward to Marseille! After leaving train station, different interrailer approaches me asking if this is the main exit, it was close by so we went there for the view of the city. While chatting, multiple strangers approached us asking for drugs of some kind. Ugh, that does not bode well... Turns out fellow interrailer goes to Toulon, a city that I'll go through in roughly 9h. For a moment there's a shed of hope I may have a shelter for a night, at strangers Airbnb. Sadly that's not the case as their group didn't all know each other entirely already.

![](marseille_night.jpg)

Staying in Marseille that is. Where I go hunt for dinner. Because we've chatted for almost an hour, everything was already either closed or was closing. Eh, there goes my hopes for trying local cuisine.

![](marseille_grafitti.jpg)

![](marseille_sculpture.jpg)

![](marseille_street.jpg)

![](marseille_alley.jpg)

So I go to an open Egyptian place that's still open where I'm recommended a sort of a sandwich with falafels. I'm also getting a for dessert (gotta survive until 6am after all!)

![](egyptian_place.jpg)

Afterwards I went to Bar Jeux where I've chatted with the barman until they've closed around 1am

![](night_street.jpg)

![](marseille_building.jpg)

Stroll through the city and onward... I went to one of the hostels with 24h reception to ask if I could wait there for a few hours as there was no point in staying the night, as it's too expensive and I'm departing at 5:57 to Milano. They said it's was fine so phew, no need of wandering around the city
