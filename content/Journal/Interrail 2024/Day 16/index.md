We went to Chocolatería San Ginés for breakfast, where I got lowly crossaint-toast with ham and cheese and porras with chocolate, while one friend got fully fledged scrambled eggs with bacon, bread, and something more. Gotta admit, chocolate was quite nice.

![](churros.jpg)

![](IMG_20240903_111513876.jpg)

Then we stumbled upon walk tour at Botin restaurant to which we joined. Tour guide surprised me and asked if we're going to stick around, and said it's fine if we do. Now we also know why there are these white-blue-red columns near barbers 😎 (they represent bandages, blood and veins as barbers were also moonlighting as surgeons and dentists back in the day)

![](IMG_20240903_114743645.jpg)

![](umbrella.jpg)

![](madrid_panorama.jpg)

![](IMG_20240903_132525616.jpg)

After the tour, we went to Templo de Debod only to find out we can't go in without reservation and that there's none available for today anymore.

![](sun_temple.jpg)

We split as one friend went back to hostel, while we met another traveller near panorama spot who heard we were speaking in Polish.

![](gardens.jpg)

We found garden nearby to which we went to, and where I may or may not have climbed onto a rooftop and may have took some photos 😎

![](madrid_street_above.jpg)

Afterwards, we met again at El Corte Inglés cafeteria at the top floor for Times-Square-like view.

![](mercado_de_san_miguel.jpg)

After that, we went to Mercado de San Miguel and proceeded to start looking for something to eat. Should've known it would become a problem as we couldn't agree on anything (or like, find something which entire group would agree on), one friend got mad and decided to go to first restaurant nearby, while we went slightly further away (like less than 200m away) where I got Paella and second friend Patatas Bravas at Café de los Austrias

![](IMG_20240903_174509075.jpg)

Sadly, that was the bone of contention in our group as afterwards second friend decided to rethink his choices. That left me alone once again to explore the city by myself.

![](madrid_streets.jpg)

So I went to check a few places like La Violeta, but it seemed to me that it's more of a gift shop rather than a place I could try these Violet sweets so I decided to move on to the next stop: La Latina Neighbourhood. 

![](sunset_madrid.jpg)

On my way there I've passed through Embajadores which felt like Brooklyn of Madrid in a way. And I've also stopped by at the Mercado de la Cebada, sadly it was closing already.

![](mercado_de_la_cebada.jpg)

![](sad_happy.jpg)

On my way back to the hostel, offer from Pura Delicia caught my eye and after shopkeeper saw I was looking at what they have, she brought me samples to try. Ice creams were... Eh, okay-ish, but what really caught my eye was fruit's dessert which I could compose by picking ingredients. There were few miscommunications there but after all, we managed to reach a consensus :)

![](IMG_20240903_203902796.jpg)

Then I went to check one TGTG place and arrived after it was sold out so... No cheap bacadillios for me tonight. Hostel it is.

After arriving, I went to the terrace bar, where I met someone at the elevator who told me it's closed which surprised, went anyway to find out it's open, though a bit windy. Chilled there for a moment and had to leave as they were closing. Huh. At 23.

![](night_sky.jpg)

So, in anyway, regroup with my friends and turns out their plans have changed. I'm back on my own from the next day.

![](portal.jpg)
