After last night of not sleeping, I finally managed to get some rest at last. I was woken up by someone's alarm at at like 8 am. That didn't mean I stayed up. Back to slumber I go until my alarm wakes me up. Or a few of them. 

Initially I thought about getting up before 9 am, but ultimately, I slept til 9:20. I did book breakfast at the hostel, which was serving up until 10, so I had to get up finally. I mean, getting that breakfast might be useful for a day, considering the costs and everything.

It was your regular continental buffet, so pretty similar to what was there in Amsterdam. Though, a regional twist was that there was a lot of croissants and other patissaries there as well. After the breakfast, I considered going back to sleep. I mean, no plan, and I was a bit beat after yesterday. My little nap ended after 15 minutes or so. 

Since train station is on the other side of the street, I figured to go there and check how my next trip would look like. There, I've found connection to Lyon and then to Barcelona which was cheaper (twice 10€) than straight one between Paris and Barcelona (34€).

Having that out of the way, I set out to explore and go to Louvre. I passed by the "Team USA house" and some seriously long queue going around the corner to Omusushi restaurant. It kinda reminded me of the queue to the breakfast place on the last day in Amsterdam. Except it was waay longer. Not far further, I also passed some very French guy casually eating a baguette. Yup, I'm in Paris for sure. 

![Empty Louvre|Top|1000x500](louvre.jpg)

I made it to the Louvre, and everything seem great, though, there are not that many people either. But hey, I can take photos and people are not going to disturb me that much, right? 

I took a stroll around the garden nearby, a lot of people were chilling there in the shade of hedgerows, some were playing with dogs, someone was busking at the arch there and one couple even had some engagement or a wedding photo ops. Your regular main park-square scenery.

Park next to the garden however, with the giant ball was closed off due to Olympics. Wait a second, I thought Olympics ended few weeks ago?

Circling back, there were some restaurants on the other side of the garden, and some people were reading under a tree that was on some sort of a hilltop.

Closeby, there were stairs leading underground. It turned out to be some sort of an entrance to something like a service area below. It also smelled like someone was using this place a toilet on a regular basis. Just some casual urban explorarion in a public space.

After a while, I also found an entrance to the underground shopping center below the Louvre, Carrousel du Louvre, they called it. Well, it had boutiques and, like, all those souvenir shops there. It was leading under the pyramid right to the entrance of the Louvre.
I was there at like 2 pm. and that's when I found out, why there are almost no people: The Louvre is closed on Tuesday... That explains a lot.

So the Louvre is not cutting it so what else I have left? An Eiffel tower! Right, so that's where I'll go I guess. On my way, passed some of the Olympics areas and souvenir shops with stuff like human-size miniature Eiffel Tower with the Olympic logo.

I had couple pins for restaurants on my map that were on the way. Closed. Classic. Another one? Not really the kind of restaurant for my budget range I'm afraid. I just walked forward, passed some modern museum on the way and then, there seemed to be some embassy of sorts or a governmental building. It was a little before 3 pm. A street or two further from said building, there was a policeman standing on the corner that decided to stop me and search my bag. Again. 

"Bonjour"

"Bonjour..." 

"Can you show me what's in your bag?" 

Oh boy... here we go. When I opened the bag he declared that he can't see what's inside so I have to pull it out.

Ok, so I'm pulling my stuff out: Water bottle, a charger with a USB cable, headphones. As I was holding everything in hands, I told him that all that is left inside is a jacket.

He said that, well, he can't see if it's just that, so I have to take it out as well. 

Hmm... Okay but I have like two hands full already, right? A bottle with a charger by the usb cable in one and bag in the other. As the cable slipped out of my hand and charger hit the ground, I asked "Can you hold this?" while giving him the bottle into the hand. Not sure if it surprised him or not but he did comply and held it, although he didn't seem entirely happy about how the tables have turned. 

> NOW'S THE TIME BOYS, HE'S DISTRACTED.

So I pulled the jacket ~~and hidden weaponry, they had no idea what hit them, as I pulled John Wick move, cleaning the street from anyone. Taking semi auto from body still holding my bottle, and taken my target~~ out. 

> From the security perspective, brother, you're asking a stranger to open their bag and someone gives you a thing to hold into your hand so you can't really do anything about whatever is inside. Like, like bro... isn't that a tad bit, you know, SecOps issue? 
> Not even from the immediate threat perspective, but the delayed one too - bottle was made of metal, and if he had no gloves on hands, that meant he left biometric data too.

They nodded, I packed jacket back, reclaimed my bottle, and picked things from the ground to pack rest of the things, and went on about my day. Charger seemed mostly fine fortunately.

While walking, I checked messages, and one friend messaged an hour earlier surprised that I haven't shared any update yet today. Oh, an update? Nothing really happened until now as police stopped me. Again.

Perhaps I was looking suspicious? Yesterday I get it: I didn't sleep and probably looked like some junkie. But today? Arafatka around neck, black drawstring bag on the back and a cord between my belt and my phone. Perhaps I did look suspicious...

So that's the second encounter with the police for one trip to France! I mean people did tell me that France is dangerous and all but I didn't know it would be in form of being stopped by police as a white person in Europe.

I was near the Arc de Triomphe. Exactly what kind of detour did I take on the way to the Eiffle Tower? On the pedestrian crossing, I saw some people taking photos near the signal lights. So I thought, hey, it must be a really good spot to take a picture, right? So I walked a little further from the crossing, toward there and not far further another police officer calls after me: "Ay! Go Back!"

Is that going to be like the third encounter with the police in two days? Thankfully, they didn't stop me to search again or anything like that. I just wasn't supposed to go any further as they most likely thought I was approaching another officer that was controlling the traffic.

I went further and was looking for a way to approach the arch as it was in the middle of the roundabout of sorts. There didn't seem to be any pedestrain crossing or other street-level approach so I looked for an underground entrance. In the tunnel there was a queue and you were supposed to buy a ticket. 

Thankfully there was also this little note that the entrance fee is free for people under certain age. I'm just glad I took this trip this year and didn't wait up any longer as that meant I could go there for free. All I had to do was to show a proof of my birthday like an ID card.

No one was checking any tickets whatsoever once I arrived at the street level. As it turns out, the area around the arch wasn't ticketed, only the entry to the arch itself.
Instead of going straight in, I took some photos around the place, then I found an entrance. Do I even want...? already got a "ticket", come on, no turning back now.

Security gate seemed rather downsized compared to other places. A simple metal detector and a person looking at the bag content after opening. And of course someone checking the tickets.

There was a rather long staircase up. Inside on the lower floor of the arch, at the top of the stairs, there was a mini museum of sorts, with a few paintings, a bunch of sculptures, and mostly miniatures of the arch itself. There was also a way further up. I made it to the top at around 4 pm.

![City panorama from the arch||1000x](arch_panorama.jpg)

The sky was crystal clear, no cloud whatsoever, save it for a few on an eastern horizon. 
I've attempted to approach the viewpoint at the edge, however the floor was incredibly slippery. Like, seriously slippery. So I slipped and fell. No, not off the arch, no. But I did fell on my back and it reminded me of an injury from few months back. 

My phone fell a little further from me, but thanks to the cord I had, it didn't go far away, so I wasn't really worried about it at the moment. I was mostly a little surprised so I proceeeded to run self-check analysis scripts while recomposing myself. Still, though, some people helped me get up and one even pointed to my phone on the floor, that was next to me that it's there.

Realistically speaking, the most painful part was ~~stain on glory~~ landing on the bottle in the bag on my back. How did I fell exactly? I suppose I attempted to like boost myself by slightly "jumping" from the floor toward the curb's viewpoint. 

![View at the Eiffel from the arch|Center|1000x500](eiffel_from_arch.jpg)

I managed to take some photos of the panorama and even the Eiffel Tower as well as the panorama of skyscrapers in the distance which put the whole city into a perspective.

![Slippery Floor warning sign on the floor||1000x400](slippery_floor.jpg)

On my way out, after like 10 or 15 minutes... I went to the exit and I saw something that would have saved me, had I known, had I seen it... when I was entering: which was a slippery floor warning written on the floor. There was even a freaking yellow sign next to the staircase that was saying attention, slippery floor. You know, all is cool, but was that actually on the entrance or is it only on the exit? Because the stairs are one way. So I went back to the entrance and there was indeed this sign saying attention and the yellow sign but it was like tucked on the side, behind the handrail. What about the text written on the floor? Well, it was there, but it was half scraped. I mean, there was some reminder of the slippery floor word, but it was like: "yeah, you know, I'm here, watch out. But, you know, I'm not really here either." 

Afterwards, I descended from the arch. On the way, at a staircase there were bars, through whicih one could get a peak to some back room or storage areas. Nothing much. I proceeded to leave and through the tunnel out of arch area.

I considered getting something to eat. There were couple of restaurants around, and I even considered stopping by at one for some dessert as someone else's order looked rather nice. But... it's close to 5 pm and I should eat something more nutritiounal rather than sugar or ice cream... even though the weather was basically begging for some ice cream. I just kept walking and some of the places on the map that I had found nearby. Nothing really caught my attention. 

So I went to the Eiffel Tower's vantage point next to the National Theater. There was small security there with a few relatively bored security guards. Slow day huh? Or it was simply 5pm and their shift was about to end.

Next, went to the Eiffel Tower, circling back already as I've reached furthest point from the hostel. So what can the person do? More photos on the way, obviously. There was this bridge in front of the Eiffel Tower that was closed with stone blocks, blocking the way for the cars. I'm not sure if it was closed in general, or if it was just closed for the Olympics. 

![Eiffel Tower](eiffel_from_below.jpg)

I attempted to try taking some photos of the Eiffle Tower there in the middle, however someone was currently in the selfie session. Had to wait a couple of minutes for the spot as their session was taking a surprisingly long while... 

Oh, hey, there are street lights! And they are signaling red currently. So I suppose the street was actually closed for the Olympics rather than being usually closed. There was the park around the Eiffel Tower too.

At the feet of Eiffel's tower, near the gates, it was almost 6 pm. I was considering going on the Eiffel Tower but it was still a few hours before sunset and I couldn't really catch this so-called golden hour to take photos so I decided to just come back tomorrow, as I was also hungry currently. Other than that, I was also running low on the battery. 

So I went on and searched for some restaurants on my way and finally I found one 40 minutes later. Not a place to eat, but a fancy sounding dessert Acai Bowl at Pavillon caught my attention. They were also fine with me charging the phone.

I got something that resembled a yogurt of sorts. I was sort of confused because I thought it would be something more frozen like an ice cream, at least according to a description. It had a bunch of fruits like kiwi and banana with a crumble on top of it too. Fruits were alright however the yoghurt tasted like... melted ice cream? Let's hope it's meant to be that way and it's more like a yogurt and it's melted... ~~please let it be a yogurt and not a melted ice cream because I believe you don't want to eat melted ice cream.~~ Not worth it. 

With food, They gave me jug of water, which I drank. What I didn't expect was them to actually refill and swap it out for the new, full one. Which I also drank... because I was kind of thirsty, right? I mean entire day, sun and everything. Everything is great. I just drank 2 litres of water.

After like an hour there, I went out, towards the hostel.

![Eiffel tower in the distance||1000x600](paris_street.jpg)

It was around 8 pm and I was passing by through a park of sorts. People were chilling on the grass, doing picnic, playing some sport games together or just chatting or studying. 
As I kept on walking, one thing became apparent: I just drank two liters of water. I also haven't used toilet at the restaurant and I was roughly 6km out of the hostel. Fun stuff right?

"I'll be strong, I'll make it to the hostel" is what I thought half an hour later when I was passing some street and people were running, or jogging. Eh, I should also do some running and maybe also run... but then again, I can't right now because of... my past that came to haunt me. So, I crossed some more streets, passed some restaurants at which I thought about stopping by and maybe using toilet there. But I didn't want to spend more money right now.

I was on the bridge at Seine, near Louvre. Wait a second, near Louvre? They had these boutiques underground, and there was the food court. They had to have the toilets down there. Let's go there! 

I made it around 10 minutes before 9 pm. Can i make it? If all else fails, there was this entrance to the service area that coincidently also smelled like some toilet, so it was a possibilty as the absolutely last resort if nothing else pans out. I know how it sounds like, but I was a bit desperate at the moment.

Once I arrived at the gardens near Louvre, I passed by a restaurant there. I was at my end so I asked the staff if I could use their toilet. One person told me to go inside. Inside as I walked there, other person from the staff told me "no, you cannot use the toilet, however you can go like 5 minutes that direction and there is a toilet down there in the in the underground", okay, five minutes walk he said, okay, maybe I'll make it. So I go down there and there indeed is a toilet. 

What I find however, is that it's closed. They were closing at around 8 pm or so. Oh no. Okay maybe something else? I'm a little bit panicked and actually considering using the last resort, that I found earlier. I honestly didn't want to do that because if someone finds out that's probably not going to end well.

I'm going to the food court area as perhaps one of the restaurants there have some toilets of their own? While walking, there's a group of three ladies coming from the other direction and asking me if I know about any toilet nearby. 

Oh, I'm not the only one in a dire need it seems! I'm telling them that I just been to one and at least men's was closed, and that I am also looking for one and I'm going to check if there's any near restaurants.

However, everything was closing already, and places there were all fast food-like and didn't have dedicated areas, so they didn't really have toilet either. Once I was considering best route to the service area, I once again met that group of ladies, in front of a closing McDonald's as they were asking staff that was sweeping the floor. They were trying to convince him to let us use staff's toilet as public one was closed. He was very hesitant about that but as the girls were seriously pleading that we were really desperate. He finally caved in and and let us in to the back rooms to the service area.

We took the service elevator down and then walked through some back room tunnel. He showed us the staff toilets. Okay that's better than nothing but, holy... those toilets were worse than... okay I'm not questioning their work ethics or anything but they could really use some cleaning there. 

Afterwards, the sink to wash hands was barely working, and hand sanitizer seemed to be on it's last breath not to mention lack of anything to dry afterwards. Water will have to do I'm afraid. I haven't seen anyone in the service tunnel either. I walked to the elevator and pressed button to go up. On the way, I thanked the guy for letting me use it and was on my way.

After finally resolving the issue at hand caused by my utter lack of forethought for a situation like that when I was drinking the water earlier, I could finally get back to... anything at around 9 pm. 

I was on my way to the hostel, and everything would be okay. If not for the fact that my belly decided to mention that the dessert that I ate wasn't really... too good. I have no idea if it was the yogurt or whether they just melted the ice cream, but I felt like I needed to use a toilet once again for the other purposes. So it was kinda, I guess, you know, what "kind" of a day... or the evening.

On the way, one guy in some orange hoodie that was walking in front of me, suddently took trash can and threw it onto the street. A short while after, a shopkeeper went out and was shouting "Oi! Who did that!"

I made it into the hostel at around 9:30 pm. I was a bit hungry and was afraid the bar might close later, so I ordered myself Chicken Caesar Salad, which was a little bit better than the previous day's dinner. 10 PM. Okay I've put it for long enough already and I'm afraid I have to actually go for that toilet now. 

In general, after the first entire day in Paris, I was wondering whether I should stay in Paris longer or not. I booked a hostel up until tomorrow, and I was wondering whether I should stay until longer, until day after or whether to go to Barcelona. What I had left to do was to check out Louvre and go to the Eiffel Tower. If it's free, like the Trumphant Arch. Which means the only thing I wouldn't have visited from the places I thought about in terms of ones "that if you're only Paris you got to visit or do" would be catacumbs and baguettes on the breakfast.

What I also found surprising was that, Paris itself is surprisingly small. It was like 10 kilometers from one edge of the city to the other. I did make like 6 kilometers and was like already on almost the other side of the city map so yeah. Well either way, That concludes the day as nothing left was left. I went to sleep roughly 40 minutes past midnight, as I was still checking out the weather in Spain and making my mind.
