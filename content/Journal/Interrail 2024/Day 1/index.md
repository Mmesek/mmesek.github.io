# Let's begin!
Before going to sleep night before, I've purchased Interrail pass and tried to buy "mandatory" seat reservation between Warsaw and Berlin... but as it turned out, they were all sold out. Last minute huh? 

That was at 3am, and I woke up at 7am with a train at 8am. Cutting it *very* short from the get go.
On my way from the house I also grabbed a hoodie as an absolutely last-second thing to pack into the backpack on the train. I was totally prepared. Trust. That's being Prepared 101.

On the train station, as I was slightly panicked and a little bit worried, I went to the ticket office to ask, to maybe get the reservation I couldn't get earlier or to just find out if I still could board that train anyway. The only issue was, I might have been slightly out of breath (Did I mention I had at that moment roughly 5 minutes until train departure?) - couldn't get reservation but still can board. Alright, that's good.

First train? TLK to Warsaw. Floor seems pretty chill here, though A LOT of people seem to go to the toilet...  

Second train? EC to Berlin. Seems pretty regular, like other PKP IC ones. 
After boarding the train, I was slightly surprised and relived there was barely anyone else, even another passenger said it's pretty empty. Well, it was it's first station, and it wasn't even Warsaw Central. I've approached conductor - who was very friendly, which was a nice sign - to ask about reservation, and thankfully, he basically "shrugged lack of it off". Phew, needless worry.

![Riding in rain](rain-outside.jpg "Bit rainy outside")

Fear that entire train is going to be occupied as the website was saying turned out unnecessary as well: at first I've sat in an empty compartment, which filled fully on the very next station in Warsaw so I had to move to the next one. This one however had two unoccupied seats out of six for the entire journey with only a single other passenger also without a ticket. Sold out, huh?

After a while, PKP even gave us a free water, which took me by surprise as I sat in second class. Totally not prepared, I had issues stashing it in my backpack, though after a few attempts and... not necessarily closing it entirely made it. Why not drink it right away? Well... I wasn't sure what I'm going to need later on, better to be prepared, right?

![Berlin's Train Station](berlin-hbf.jpg "Berlin!")

Once I got to Berlin... Darn, that's a big train station. Alright so where is train to Hamburg... Wait a second, there are MULTIPLE FLOORS WITH DIFFERENT TRACKS?! Oh boy, that complicates things...
Got like... less than 10 minutes to catch the train, right? Alright, there's a weird monitor with departures... Found it! Now... where is the track... Lost a little, maybe. Not much though. Ok, got it. Made it on board!

Third train, Berlin-Hamburg was neatest of all so far. Is this second class? Daarn... It's like if each train was an upgrade over last one... well, almost, as seats were a little narrower than on previous one, and probably most boring. Sadly, I've selected wrong destination station, the "Harburg" one. As a result left one station too early, having to board another regional train to get to Hbf one.

Overall, for the first travel day using the pass, it was stress-free save it for the lack of "mandatory" reservation that was "sold-out". Flexibility of only showing one ticket for the entire day and just entering any train is also pretty nice. Not to mention it sort of saved me from a mistake (even though no tickets were checked on that last part)

![Hamburg's Train Station](hamburg-hbf.jpg "Hamburg Hbf")
# After reaching Hamburg
I've set to go to eat something at Erdapfel. There, after some miscommunication I've got their "Aristotle", which was a potato with olives, feta cheese, some beans and sour-cream for 10.9€. Aaand that was my only food besides breakfast for the day. Off to the great start, right?

![Potatoes with vegetables and sour cream||700x500](erdapfel.jpg "Aristotle Potatoes from Erdapfel")

Took a stroll around city in an opposite direction than one mentioned by my host, went under the bridge and... found a group of people taking a photo... let's join! Walked a bit more towards the address where Diehalle was supposed to be on the map, didn't find anything matching the description and later found out it was bit further down the road.

![3 other people taking a photo underbridge||800](photography-session.jpg "Oh hey, are you taking a photo as well?")

Then walk back towards the "old-town" city square where I was supposed to meet with my host, and then to the pub where host was waiting for me because I was late. But hey, at least the place wasn't... too hard to find (just a tad).

![Totally no joints here||750](bar.jpg "Just chilling")
# Accommodation
Using public transportation totally-not-without-a-ticket with my host, we reach their place. There, I find that host's room is roughly 2x2m2 with one mattress that *could be shared*. I mean... I knew this trip is going to be a new experience but that escalated quickly... Thankfully, there's a salon in a basement with a coach. 

Ok, that actually rocks. Even if there's a threat of someone coming in and I'll need to evacuate in like moments because no one is actually supposed to sleep there. WELL... Oh, and obviously, I can't let myself in because doors can only be opened by a key or from the inside, and shared toilet is upstairs. Eh, seems like a rough couple of nights.
