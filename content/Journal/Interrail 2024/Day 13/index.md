>I've been taking roads less taken  
To find my way to you

2nd day in Barcelona! Started by chatting at a hostel with a roommate, and then moved on to look for a place to eat breakfast - stopped by at Faire Brunch and got Egg Benedict with Avocado and a cookie. Egg was quite well done, cookie wasn't too much up my alley.

![Emergency Exit doors](evacuation_exit.jpg)

Next stop: Park Guell's vantage point if it's free as internet suggested.
On my way there had to make a pitstop for a toilet at a cafe/bakery, and... Third time I had to brush off my Spanish (translator). I've been told the direction and bought Magdalena cupcake afterwards. Turns out it's just a regular lemon cupcake I had in my country. Eh... At least that was only 85 cents

![Panorama of the city](barcelona_panorama.jpg)

There, I've thought to myself "stick with me, and we'll go to ? places"
find ourselves where we wouldn't think to go", if only I had someone to go there with... #WIP

Hiked a little behind a park, found Barcelona's panorama and then fell while climbing down. 

![Some trees, and bushes](wilderness%20near%20Barcelona.jpg)

From there I went to Parc del Laberint d'Horta, and while trying to locate the entrance met a family partially from Poland from whom I've got Spanish language school card once we got to the entrance.

![Dedication plaque](plaque.jpg)

![Engraving of love birds on the tree](tree_engravings.jpg)

![Parc del Laberint d'Horta](labyrinth_park.jpg)

![Labyrinth made out of hedgerows](labyrinth_closeup.jpg)

Afterwards, I was supposed to meet with a roommate at Sagarda Familia before the mass for a free entrance, however once I got there, I felt way too hungry to make it through so had to leave before it even started.

![Interior of Sagrada Familia](sagrada_familia_inside.jpg)

Then, after reaching hostel, I had like 7% of battery remaining, yay to not having a powerbank. At the room, I've chatted with few othe roommates, and went to get chicken fingers at the bar hoping they are going to be enough. Sadly they weren't so I started chatting at a bar, met one expat from Denmark who mentioned Angel Numbers, and met another traveler who knew a place that was still open.

So we went to Vinitus for tapas! With a little problem: I had no idea what tapas are, besides the fact it's a dish served on bread. So I was wondering about few options. All of which were not tapas, even though they were under tapas menu. So I went with... Chicken. Stripes. Again.

![Bunch of tapas at the restaurant](tapas.jpg)

Desperate for something else, also added churros to the mix. Too bad they were bit... Mid. Chocolate was nice though.

Back to the hostel. Pub crawl happening! Went to my room to leave the bag and went downstairs thinking people are already leaving so I went with them thinking it's our group. I've asked and they confirmed though I saw no one familiar. They said I look like... Well, exactly how I guess I looked like: influencer, probably a game streamer who deals pot.
So this one guy trolls one of the lady that we are brothers, I'm adding that of two different mothers, I think they are stone drunk because she seems to actually buy it for a second....

At the first pub I decided to evacuate myself, then on the way back I meet organizer from the hostel who tells me pub crawl is canceled because people wanted to hang out at the rooftop. Well... A little too late.

![City street at night](night_barcelona.jpg)

Then, while walking streets at night decided to take some photos... Almost one of worst mistakes: there's one guy that sees I'm filming, and approaches me, asks where I'm from, and wants to see last recording. Which I thankfully messed up and the only thing that was recorded was how I hide my phone away.
