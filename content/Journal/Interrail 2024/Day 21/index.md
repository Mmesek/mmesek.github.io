After going out last night, and being back a little before 4am, woke up 6:40, packed and found out that guy from night shift managed to prepare breakfast earlier than 7:30 after all (before going to sleep, I've asked if it could be possible to have it earlier, he said that not really as it's a busy day)

So, with this turn of events I've had few sweet pastries with jam and some hazelnut cream, as well as managed to chug a toast and juice under 10min, should get me going for a bit, hopefully

Yeeaaah.... That means it's 7:30 and my train departs 8:09. Nearest metro station? Gotta run. Thanks for breakfast though! When I get to the metro, I'm new to the whole "pay with your paying card to enter" kinda thing so I'm slightly worried but all in all it works out, and I'm looking for the correct line... Aaaand turns out I didn't need to run, next metro in 5m

7:42, bit worried I won't make it. But in the end, train station reached at 7:58, phew, 10minutes to figure it out

![](train_station_grafitti.jpg)

Where do I find departures... 8:03 there! Line 5! Where is line 5? That direction. So I go. back to the start, Line 1...
So going back once again, line 5! 8:05
Is this the correct line? Yup! Train arrived at 8:07, phew. First available car? Is this first class? Wait, are you guys also interrailing?

![](lisbon_train_station.jpg)

Conductor: Oi! What's your car number? Riiight, it was first class. So on to the very end we go. Just for 2 stations... Aaaand the train has a delay... And conductor actually came to the very end...

So we arrive at first connection... After a delay. Thankfully, regional train waited for us as there were multiple (5!) interrailers switching trains there

![](badajoz_platform.jpg)

At Badajoz we find out that seats between Merida and Madrid are sold out. Whoops. That complicates things a bit. However, I've met another traveller that was going to Sevilla, so I'm like... Huh, what the hack, let's go there as well! I'm glad I had one unused travel day so I could do that though

![](badajoz_bridge.jpg)

![](badajoz_river.jpg)

So, instead of planned Valencia tonight, Seville it is, seat reservations seems to be also cheaper than if I would go through Madrid. And on top of that? No need to go from Atocha to Chamartin

Light breakfast and no refueling at Badajoz means another chocolate bar from the backpack. Couldn't find it at first because it was sooo squished, so I played inventory management game, and then found out the first thing I pulled out was what I was looking for

![](sevilla_platform.jpg)

After arriving at Seville, I've set out to find something to eat, went for local tapas at Taberna Águilas where at first I was charged less which confused me enough for the waiter to check again and then took a stroll around the city towards hostel.
