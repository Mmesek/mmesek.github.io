After getting to Katowice, I've been tasked with an escort elder quest. We've went to ticket office to ask for seat reservations for EIP trains since I had doubts if we can board without it after what I've read online. There, they told us to go to customer service.

At customer service we've received seat reservations without any problems due to flood on the last part of our journey. Then we've went to eat something nearby. Since I'm trying local cuisine in the countries I visit, pierogi ruskie and gołąbki were the only fitting things there. 

![I failed miserably to take photo of mine, so I took one of stranger's (by their permission!)](piergoi.jpg)

![](katowice_platform.jpg)
Afterwards we went to the platform, and after boarding train, I've realized I'm on the wrong side, so I had to go through entire one. There, at the restaurant section, who do I meet? Rest of the group we left back in Ostrava! They found another car 10 minutes later and were lost for 2h, but ultimately managed to find a train and then hopped on to one to Warsaw at the last moment as well.

![](free_water.jpg)

On the train, they gave away water once again, this time with choice between carbonated or not. Ahh, welcome back Polish trains!

![](warsaw_station.jpg)

After reaching Warsaw I went to eat something at the eastern train station (vegetable green tortilla wrap, very Polish I know) while waiting for sister to come and deliver me keys to home.

Then I've boarded last train on this trip to Lublin and the only thing that remains is to summarise entire trip
