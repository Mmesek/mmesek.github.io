Not sure if I've slept on the train station much, but got much more sleep on the next two trains considering how often I was waking up thinking this is it.

![](verona_porta_nueva_train_station.jpg)

At the first connection stop, I've had crossaint for a small breakfast

![](snowy_alps.jpg)

After reaching Brennero Brenner I was caught by surprise that it was snowing. AND I ONLY HAVE SHORTS. 

![](snow_in_september.jpg)

I've found some respite at a cafe on the other side of the road where I had panini italiano with hot chocolate for a second breakfast. 

![](hot_chocolate.jpg)

However... I've failed to locate platform from which my next train was departing and I was too late to board it. From some reason I've started running hoping I can catch it last minute only to fall and bruise myself in the colds of Alps.

![2 months later, I still have scars from this event](wounds.jpg)

Took the very next train out of this place, one that was going to Munich as it was also passing through Innsbruck.

![](snowing.jpg)

It was late, instead of changing at Innsbruck I've went straight to Munich. On the train, I've met friendly Italians who gave me some wet tissues so I could clear out my wounds. Turns out running for train at near zero degrees, leaves you bruised... a lot

![](munich_o.jpg)

![](munich_sculpture.jpg)

At Munich I've went to Steinheil Sechzehn where I had Rattatouille. On the way there, I've also grabbed snack pretzel from bakery. 

Not perfect but it'll do. It started raining, however kind stranger allowed me to shelter under their umbrella as we were going in more of less same direction. There, I've learned that my next two destinations are in danger zone due to weather conditions. 

![](ratatouille.jpg)

After reaching place I was going to, I've received a notification from Interail that my travel might be disrupted by weather conditions... Not a good sign. 

![Dog I've passed seemed to share my sentiment](wet_dog.jpg)

Broken, at the verge of breakdown, I've started checking connections... Everything seem to continue to operate... Here's hoping.

![](puddle_photography.jpg)

![](anonymous_again.jpg)

![](munich.jpg)

Then... It kept raining. Found shelter at Rischart where I had apfelschenecken. 
Afterwards, I've went to look for a restaurant for dinner. I went to first one I've found, turned out it's a creperie. Great, so I'm considering between the two and ask waiter for recommendation between them. 

![](pancake.jpg)

Worst mistake I could make next to not taking long pants on this trip: I got recommended one that shared name with what I wanted.... BUT it wasn't from the ones I considered. Instead of sweet one, I got savory one, which usually isn't a problem, but in this instance it was. Not only due to cost but also due to the fact it simply wasn't good. Vegetables were already in Rattatouille earlier but these felt heavier.

Eh, whatever. Someone at the table next to mine allowed me to use their powerbank so I managed to squeeze some % just in case while eating.

![](beer_on_train.jpg)

Back to the train station, where I stop by one of bakeries to buy some supplies for the night. I linger a few minutes at one to ask if they are any good for long travel (it was a busy place as it turns out!) and in the end I got an extra crossaint at no cost too, which was unexpected but my wallet was thankful. (Even though I now realize I meant to ask for pretzel which I didn't, oh well)
Train is late, but that's Deutsch-Bahn, right?

![](station_grafitti.jpg)

Next connection: Nürmberg and then train to Prague
