Porto! This city has came up quite a few times in the past for me during researches. Now that I'm here, let's find find out what all the fuss is about.

![](famous_hostels_map.jpg)

At the hostel I've got recommendations for places to eat, so I've went to two of these places, both nearby where I got Omelete Mista which was a strange omelette with fries and salad. Somehow it didn't "feel" Portuguese but maybe that's what they eat here. After that, I've went to the hostel to check walking tour where guide told me to go to square where they'll start. Having few more minutes I've went to bakery nearby and got crossaint with... Custard of sorts? It was pretty good though

![](omlette_mista.jpg)

After securing the breakfast, I've went onto a walking tour which turned out into a walking day as I've met fellow traveller with whom we hangout afterwards

During the break during the tour, I've grabbed local pastry with various meats inside and pastel de nata at Batalha. Both it was pretty tasty

![](IMG_20240905_134043972.jpg)

![](decorated_ceiling.jpg)

![](IMG_20240905_145243116.jpg)

Dance-off

After the tour, We went to eat something, but went too far!

![](old_stairs.jpg)

So what do we do? We take the stairs. On the stairs? The further we go, the less taken this path is. 

![](river_panorama.jpg)

Once we found ourselves in a place that seemed like taken out of a different place, I recalled thinking to myself few days [earlier](../Day 13) about that I didn't have anyone to go to strange places with... well, now I had, and all I could comment on that was "stick with me, and we'll end up where none of us would [even think to go?] otherwise". #wip

Ultimately we get a view we wouldn't get if we didn't get lost. There we continue a climb to something that looks abandoned or like someone's backyard. So we back off and try a different route... Which seems to lead to nowhere! Then we see people coming from the backyard and we try it again as they told us it leads to a street.

![](underpass.jpg)

Then we went for a "lunch" at Officina Dr Rissoiso. After that, we strolled around the city and decided to go towards the ocean

![](porto_tree.jpg)

On the way to the ocean we take a detour through the park in which there was book fair of sorts. After finally finding a way out of the park (the way we came through)

This place is beautiful. This side of Europe seem to have specific shade of green. 

![](porto_sun.jpg)

![](tunnel_romantico.jpg)

There, we found ourselfs in Caminhos do Romântico. After accidentally walking into someone's backyard finally of course

![](bridge_sunset.jpg)

When we were near the river at the street level, we saw stairs that lead us below, to the water level where we walked rest of the distance at. Afterwards, we climbed the ladder to get out of there

![](ocean_sunset.jpg)

We made it right for the sunset toward the furthest point of the pier. Then we walked all the way back to the city, along the way we stopped at the fancy restaurant where they had some Port Wine and food

![](bridge_night.jpg)

![](porto_wine.jpg)

After dinner (and charging our phones!) we went back to the hostels and after a while I went to the train station to go to Lisbon overnight

![](tower_hostel.jpg)

![](regional_night_train.jpg)

On a train, I had some doubts. Why exactly did I change my plans? Porto seems like a city worth staying in for a while longer... I even found people to hang out with, basically right off the bat too... Oh right, my budget. I shouldn't blow it up at the begining I suppose...

While there weren't many people around I kept constantly waking up through the ride. Some choices...
