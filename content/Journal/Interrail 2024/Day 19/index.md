After leaving the train, I thought I could use suburban trains - wrong, they require card and I only had a QR code. Thankfully, some strangers allowed me to enter with their card and leave by piggybacking them

![](lisbon_station.jpg)

What surprised me, was that a lot of places were open before 7am. I've grabbed some pastry from bakery to get me through next few hours. At the hostel, I was allowed to check-in early so I crashed on the only available made bed in the dormitory thinking it was mine, to be woken up few hours later with staff telling me I crashed on the wrong bed. Whoops.

![](stairs_to_hostel.jpg)

Breakfast! Mixed Omelette (that's the same thing as yesterday's mista) and pancakes. More expensive than Porto but breakfast is important, especially after eating rather little for past few days... or so I'm telling myself to justify the price tag

![](breakfast_fries.jpg)

After breakfast, I've set to try famous Pastel de Nata's. First visit was in Manteigaria around the corner: it was hot and a little too fluid for my taste. 

![](Manteigaria_pastel_de_nata.jpg)

![](lisbon_bridge.jpg)

![](bad_decisions.jpg)


![](enjoy_now_v3.jpg)

Then I've walked toward river and then walked by riverside toward Belem where I've tried Pastel de Belem as it was roughly 4km or so of walking there, on which path I've got an ice-cream with a slushi thingi

![](sun_tree.jpg)

The place was incredibly easy to spot as there was a queue to the takeaway counter where a Canadian behind me said he likes my scarf and if I know what my arafatka is

I've also wanted to try a different pastry but that was sold out so I took their Crêpe 

Pastel was better, though also warm like previous one. Crepe on the other hand... Incredibly dried

While chilling nearby, I've got a message from traveller from yesterday that they've just arrived in Lisbon. Well, I'm 6km away from the centre... And I was running low on water... It's not going to be straightforward way back... So I took the same path I've got there to go back, and still made it later than planned

![](sunset_between.jpg)

![](pink_street.jpg)

![](rose_3d.jpg)

So we meet, first relatively big spot neither of us could miss I've found between our hostels, still one further away for me because I thought, hey, it can't be that bad... well, I was late, it was. I think I recognize them, but I'm only at 75% positive I got the right person, I say hi, and then I feel like I got wrong person... Well, it was the right one. Then I miss like 3 social cues or more. Yay to not getting much rest last night and then walking dehydrated 

![](anime_bar.jpg)

So we go to grab something to eat for dinner. After a while of stumbling around, finding out Portuguese restaurant closed its kitchen already (it was like 21:20 or so) we settled on a ramen place and got poke bowls. While leaving I'm trying to brag with my Japanese, so I good night and asked if I said it right... Only to find out the guy said he doesn't know as he doesn't speak Japanese, he's Chinese. Should've known... So we finish on a little mandarin's lesson for Arigato. In a ramen place
