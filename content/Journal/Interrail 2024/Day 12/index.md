1st day in Barcelona is about to start! Gotta find something to eat first as I didn't take breakfast at the hostel this time. 

Crêpe at a cafe. Alright start but ain't gonna cut it. Spanish tortilla with a smoothie at a brunch place nearby. That'll do.

![Sun over Sagrada Familia](sagrada_familia.jpg)

![Living statue levitating-like over the ground](living_statue.jpg)
Later on I've walked to Park Guell, to find out they only let locals walk-in and rest need to have reservation. Welp, let's head back. In our way back we stop at one of bookmarked places where they apparently have really good carrot cake. And 5 minutes earlier I'm told of Patatas Bravadas to try. And this place also serves them. Darn, they're spicy! But carrot cake... Cinnamon was good but I would say it was a bit overrated.

![Patatas Bravadas dish](patatas_bravadas.jpg)

Next stop? Mercat la Boqueria. 
This one is more of a fresh meat market rather than restaurant type of thing like in Rotterdam or Copenhagen.

![Sun over the entrance to La Boqueria market](la_boqueria.jpg)

Direction: Beach, since it's super close. 
But it can't be this close because we are taking back alleys and streets where there's almost no people and we are also visiting Santa Maria del Mar. 

![Church of Santa Maria del Mar](santa_maria_del_mar.jpg)


![Alley in Barcelona](barcelona_alley.jpg)

These alleys must be pretty during night, though I would probably prefer to take some people with me... Just in case.

![Sea](barcelona_sea.jpg)
Barceloneta... Is far more rocky than I expected. I would recommend beach next door as it has far less people and sand is much more soft.

Alright, Triumphal Arch... There's grass in Barcelona after all! 

![Arch in Barcelona](barcelona_arch.jpg)

Pitstop at the hostel to recharge battery and onto Terrace Bar to try tapas.

...where I find out I need a reservation to enter. So I go to nearby Buenos Miguel for Focaccia.

Then hitting hostel and chatting at a bar for a bit, while I just went there to refill my water bottle

Day expenses: 37.8€
