I don't think I slept at all before arrival at around 6:40 am. I pulled myself together and exited the train once it reached the platform in Lille. On the platform, I was a bit sleepy as I didn't get to sleep much, a few short and interuptted naps at best. It was also still chilly outside. I looked around and went toward the main area looking for ticket office to purchase reservation for train to Paris.

# Layover at Lille

What surprised me, was the amount of gendarmerie patroling. One group spotted me looking around, stopped me and approached. Oh-oh. What did I do this time?

> Who's the most suspicious type around if not the guy with a backpack that didn't sleep for over 24h, spent last night on the street and looks like a total wreck?

Bonjour? They've started off in French, and then switched to half English with heavy accent, probably after they've realized I was utterly confused. They've asked for my ID, asked how my surname is pronouncable, and whether I carry any illegal items; listing: any knifes, blades, weapons, drugs? No, no, nope, not any of those... (as far I was aware anyway). While questioning, person I've been speaking with was also typing something on his phone, perhaps checking my ID numbers? At one point, spoke something in French to his college, gave me back my ID and they were on their way.

Geez, I barely got to France and I was already stopped by the Police...
Okay, what about those tickets? I've went to one office, but it didn't seem like I can get reserveration there and I was directed to one of the ticket machines found throughout the station.

I've also looked for toilet, however one there was seemed paid so I looked a bit further. There was also a patisserie, and a fast food like McDonald's. Perhaps they have... Riiight, there was a sign saying they do not have any toilet. Nevermind then. Noneless, I took a seat to check train connections to Paris and prices. There was one around 10:40 am for 12€ on the app. Cheapest option as earlier train costed more than that.

I was hoping to seat for a while, rest for a bit and pass some time in the meantime. However, there were some flying monsters around so I took my leave shortly after sitting.

Next I went to  purchase the ticket at the machine, I was somewhat confused. There were no trains to Paris I was looking for. Uhh... what? A bit more searching and it turned out the machine was showing departures from Lille Flanderes and my train would be from Lille Europe. I've checked rail planner and when it shown me that there's a train that takes like 2 hours to get to Lille Europe from Lille Flanderes. I was confused. Are those two distinct cities? No, something's not right. Map shows it... circles back?

There was a young guy purchasing ticket on another machine, so I've approached him and asked if he's speaking English by any chance. I've read and heard stories of having difficulties communicating in English in France, and that they might be more hostile if I start oif in one, so I tried in French: "Parlez-vous anglais?". To which he replied with a sentence in french that involved "francais"? Uh... Oh okay, sorry, nevermind. I've backed off.

Moment later he approached me and replied in English (wait a second, was my French really that good?) that he meant he also speaks French. We chatted a bit and I've asked him whether he knows where Lille Europe is, and how far it is. He told me I have to leave the station, and go around it, and there's Lille Europe on the other side.

Wait. Leave the building? Yup. That's... Hmm.. oh okay. Thanks. 
I was confused because every train station I've been to before was as a part of one building, and if city had multiple, they were few kilometers apart.

I've downloaded map for the city, checked it, and indeed, Lille Europe was closeby yet separated. That's... strange but alright. When everything was confirmed, I went back to the machine to buy the reservation. Clicked through all the menus and with shortly after, I had a printed copy in my hand.

Once I left the building, there were like two squads of soldiers in V formation patrolling as well. That's quite a security for a train station for sure...

![Lille Flanders' train station||1000x600](lille.jpg)

I've considered my breakfast options around, though none of the restaurants really caught my attention, so I directed myself towards Lille Europe station.

# Lille Europe

When turning around a corner, it was already visible, bigger than the one I was at. It was a short walk and I still had over 2h until the train. I went looking for breakfast as well as to charge my phone just in case a little. While eating, I've also researched a little which area is unsafest in Paris, and where my hostel would be there. Du Nord. Aaand.... Du Nord. Oh great... Just great.

On the top floor there was a piano in the middle and someone was currently playing it too.

I've found a restaurant nearby, after walking around for a while, on a higher floor. 
There, I've got pancakes with honey sauce. It was a bit too sweet for me though. When choosing seat to take, I've looked for electric outlet to charge the phone as well.

During breakfast and while recharging phone, I've looked for the hostels in Paris.
I've settled on St. Christopher's Inn as it was particularly close to the train station and relatively cheap. I also thought it would be good for depature. Also visited hostel's website and found that they had towels for rent for 4€. Afterwards, I've also did a search on areas in Paris to avoid in particular. One of those places was Du Noord district. And where was the hostel located? Du Noord.

Less than 2h until the train. There were few waiting benches with some people, in the main area and after taking an extra stroll around I've sat there once I was feeling like I need to rest more than before. I tried to have some semi-lying position with a backpack however it was extremely uncomfortable so I resigned that idea.

I saw one guy, not too different age to mine, with a french mustache also waiting for a train there. I tried to keep myself awake however I started falling asleep. I took a walk and felt like passing out even when standing so I retorted back to my seat. I couldn't really fight my tired eyes off much longer, and the French mustache guy was still waiting there. 

I've approached him and asked again in French if he speaks English. He replied, with a shy smile, that he speaks a little. There was also a strong French accent. It was possible to communicate so all's good though.

I've asked where he goes. and he said to Paris. Nice, me too! 
I've presented him my pickle though:  I'm falling asleep and I don't know if I can manage to stay awake. Could you wake me up if that happens before the train? It wasn't a problem. Phew.

Soon after, I passed out on my seat and 20 minutes later the guy I asked earlier approached me to say that there's a platform for our train. Holy sh... I fell asleep. Thank you my friend. We went to look out for our platform. There, we thought we had a right place and once approached there was an officer, or train staff directing us to where to go, as the place we were was for arrivals. He was speaking French and I'm just glad I had made a French-speaking travel companion just now.

We walked to the other staircase and boarded the train few minutes later. Somehow, we even had seats row or two apart. 10 minutes later we departed, I've set an alarm before the train would arrive and after another 10 minutes the music started once again...

I woke up an hour later, thanks to the alarm. My friends were already scheming breaking me out of jail and planning our getaway as I was unreachable after messaging them about my earlier encounter with the police. Appreciated guys!

# Paris

Train arrivied, and when leaving, we've exchanged goodbyes with the guy I've met earlier as we passed each other on the way out. Hostel was pretty close to the train station, although I still routed the path on map there, just to know in which way to go out of the station. It was a throw away - on the other side of the street.

When I left the station, what hit me immedietaly was the weather. Clear sky and warm temperature. Such a contrast to the cold night and rain of past few days. Finally shorts I had were useful.

As I approached pedestrian crossing, I was immedietly hit with how much different traffic lights work. People seemed to use them as a mere suggestion. Lights itself were too short to cross the street, pedestrian lights were harder to see than car lights for pedestrians and a lot of people just crossed on red.

# Hostel

Once I got to the hostel I went to book the my stay. While booking, during a small talk I mentioned I haven't sleep for an entire day or so. I've also asked how much would it be for a towel. I didn't receive an answer, just a keycard, booking information and... a towel. Huh? But... how much? Receptionist just smiled and said it's gift. Hmm... Appreciated!

I went to the room, which was still pretty much empty, like the previous one. Although this time, there was an upgrade in luxury - there were curtains near each bed. Sweet. I proceeded to reorganize myself, and finally got to use a toilet as well. 

An hour later I've started looking into places where I could eat something decent. Nothing really caught my attention online so I took a stroll out of the hostel towards Notre Dame, hoping that I may find something on the way, couple minutes before 2 pm. While I was leaving, two girls that were checking-in right after me, were also going out. 

I thought about approaching them to ask about their plans, perhaps they got some specific place picked and I could gather some ideas where to go. Though, I didn't really feel too socializable at the moment considering my last night's sleep, or lack there of, so I went the other way. In reality, I was hesitating a little too long and the opening in their conversation passed. 

# Streets

While strolling I walked through one street that from some reason seemed like a Bombai of Paris. It was still well within the dangerous zone so... Well... At least it's a broad daylight, right?

On my way, I've encountered some of the famous Paris pixelart grafittis, as well as... homeless' matress near doors at the alove of one of the buildings?

![Mini arch in Paris||x700](mini_arch.jpg)

Once I've reached the end of the street I was on, I've arrived at the mini version of Triumphal Arch under which a passel of pidgeons seemed to have a congregation where they were chilling or sun bathing. 

I passed some museum on my way and there was a queue. Hmm, perhaps it might be worth checking out? There was young teenanger with a grandma at the end of the queue, and I felt like I might have higher chance of speaking English with younger people, considering it's the old Parisians I should be wary of according to the internet.

I've casually asked about the queue and how much the entry fee is, and she seemed eager to try English and really tried to answer. Her grandma seemed to encourage her as well. It took her a while to figure out how to say 15€. Ah, the joy of pronouncing numbers past 10 in a foreign language for the first time. Been there 🥲 It was unexpectely an amusing encounter, appreciated the effort, though!

#  Crêpes

Nothing in terms of food near the main streets caught my attention to I've strolled through some back streets, and there was a street cart serving Crêpes. I've approached and said "Bonjour", to which older crêpier replied "Bonjour" as well. I... That's where my French ends, so I rolled the dice: "Uhh... English?" Response was very simple, and prompt: "Enemies.". After that, vendor moved on to serve someone else.
What was I expecting anyway?

I continued further and found on a map restaurant with Crêpes, Crêperie Beaubour nearby. I walked there, and found myself on some square around 3 pm. I went inside, and once a waiter came, I asked if English is fine, he called a waitress who presumably spoke it. I wasn't quite sure of how cashless France is yet, especially considering Germany wasn't entirely cashless, so I asked if I can pay contactless with phone. Better safe than sorry, right? As it was alright, I decided to sit inside - it was bit cooler there. 

Once seated, and as I received French menu, I began deciphering foreign words with help of arcane tools - DeepL and Optical Character Recognition through camera. A while later, waitress came and asked if I would like an English menu. There's an English menu?! Sure!

I've settled on Crêpes Norwegian which was with Salmon and as I was wondering what syrop drink was, I also grabbed Lemon Syrop. The latter turned out to be a simple lemonade with some drop of concentrated lemon. Former however... Was a Do It Yourself edition. There was a crêpe at the bottom, on top of it were rolled slices of smoked salmon and a lemon slice. Next to it was salad, on which was a sauce.

I'll admit, I was expecting something more... inside rather than on top. Other than that, the crêpe itself was a bit... dry?

# More strolling

Shortly after I was back on my way to the Notre Dame, which I've reached around 4 pm.

![Notre Dame under reconstruction||1000x300](notre_dame.jpg)

It was under active reconstruction, after the fire few years earlier, with scaffolding all around and a couple of cranes.

I've walked a bit more and crossed the river onto the other side. There's 23°C and it somehow feels like it would be 32°C. There was a pack of English speaking people, considering topics of conversation, most likely tourists, which for a brief moment I was considering joining, but then decided against it strolled further.

![Just a tree||1000x650](tree.jpg)

Went to a park nearby, although shade there didn't deliver much respite from the undermining sun. I walked a bit further and found myself closeby to Louvre. Mixture of temperature, lack of proper rest and possibly dehydration put me back on a way to the hostel.

A little past 5 pm I was once again under the pidgeon-infested miniature Triumphal Arch, and around 5:30 pm I was at a hostel. At the room, most if not all beds already had their curtains closed. I felt like passing out so I took a nap, and a bit before 6 pm, the music started once more...

# Evening

I woke up past 8:15 pm. Few people were talking in room. There was this older voice of someone that already retired, like over 60 or 70. And two younger voices of girls from earlier. 

After checking-in, I read the rules at the bedbunk, and one of them mentioned that anyone above 40 needs to either book into single room or be in a group that books entire room. Well, interesting take on that rule I suppose. 

Anyway, the guy was saying that he was travelling for a while now and he was from England. He mentioned that he was alone at this point after his wife passed, and decided to travel. He also told the story about how he had his medicaments in the back pocket of his backpack and one time someone opened that pocket when he was sleeping while waiting for a train so he had no meds with him. He finished his story with a warning that he may act like a werewolf at night. Well... girls said they have to go, meanwhile the guy went to sleep. My time to slip out of the room! 

I went for dinner at the bar downstairs around 9 pm. Some people there were playing beer pong, while others were chatting or watching sports channel. I found an empty seat near one of the tables, and began investigating menu they had. 

What's cheap, seems meaningful and fits description of a dinner? Choice went for Cali Wrap with Nachos. Nachos were part of the meal, not an extra. Not ideal, Yeah, I get it. But it was cheap. Mostly because there was like a -25% discount for people staying at the hostel. 

![Bar in St. Christopher's Inn||1000x500](bar.jpg)

After eating I chilled for a while there, researching things to do on the next day and picking photos from the day to share on the internet. 

I thought about chatting with someone, however those that seemed chattable were playing beer-pong. Oh hey, two of them were those girls from my room. Well, I don't drink beer, and they seemed occupied with game.

Friend wrote to me regarding planning our accommodations for the rest of the trip. I thought about walking a bit so we were went on a voice call to continue.

While we were chatting, I thought about going out and walking around the street, however it was already past 10 pm and I was a bit afraid that I'm not in a safe district. So I stayed within like 50 meters from the entrance, or more specifically, of the guard at the door (Though that guy seemed more like a bouncer). Well, to be fair I didn't make it too far out before wifi dropped and my call connection hanged. Well... staying within 10m that is. 

We continued our discussion about prices and where we'll be sleeping, or more specifically, me trying to convince friend that hostel is not such a terrible idea and it's actually possible to get some sleep there. We've finished talking about backpacks. What have I done picking backpack for myself...

Afterwards at around 11pm I went to finally take the shower utilizing the opportunity given by temporarly having the towel. The toilet was shared this time so I had to figure out how to hang my clothes in a cabin without getting them wet. Fortunately, the cabin was big enough to avoid splashing any water on the hang that was by the door. Nothing along the lines of what hatted traveller from few days ago said about "wild things" was happening though.

I had a moment to chill at last, and realized It has been few days since last Friday. And usually Friday means there's new music on Spotify. So, before heading to sleep I caught up with new releases and went to sleep around 1 am.
