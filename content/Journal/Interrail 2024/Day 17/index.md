All alone once again, after 2 days with buddies instead of rest of the trip. Oh well. Happens.


Breakfast was composed of pastry I bought at supermarket Dia near hostel: two crossaints, one empanadas and one other pastry, both latter ones filled with some tomato-ish/vegetably sauce
![](IMG_20240904_090445484.jpg)

After reaching Madrid Chamartin I was greeted with a rather... Confusing station entrance as it was through the car parking. As I still had around an hour until train, I've had second breakfast composed of crossaint with Nocilla, which is apparently Spanish Nutella. That's one way of doing brand ripoffs, Spain (It was more hazelnutty though so nice)

Onto the luggage control... Where I'm stopped and asked if I have two shishash in my bag. I'm thinking "drugs? No way" so I answer that I do not think so. I'm then asked to put my bag through the scanner again and guard confirms I have two shissors in my bag, he repeats it couple of times and now I get what he meant: scissors. Yes, I do have them. So I'm pulling out the bag with them to show and so I have to go with another guard to a room to show them. Ugh. Third guard asks me to pull them out after pointing big ones, so I do, he looks at the blade and "Oh ok go on". Onto packing then... And then to find the right platform... And then to go (5min or so) through the ENTIRE platform just to reach the train

Next stop: Pontevedra... Actually scratch that, straight to Vigo since that train goes there as well. After exiting station Urzaiz straight to city panorama I was simply wowed by how colorful buildings were there. Then I've took a stroll around city, toward what I thought would be an old town, only to have serious trouble finding any restaurants. Settled on trying Empanadas de Gallega at nearby bakery

From there walked back by the harbour side to the train station Guixar where I had to make free seat reservation at a ticket office. Thankfully, queue was pretty short, though there seemed to be some problems with machine for someone before me anyway

![](portugese_train.jpg)

![](portugese_train_2.jpg)

After around 4h of layover, boarded train to Porto. Due to barely not eating anything I grabbed chocolate bar from depths of backpack I brought from home to hopefully survive until destination

![](sunset_train.jpg)

I've been thinking about my plans for Portugal. Initially, I was planning on staying in Porto for 2 days, and 1 day in Lisbona. I've realized however, that Lisbona is a capitol, so surely there would be more things to do there, right? While checking trains, I've found that there even is a night train after midnight from Porto to Lisbona that takes like 5h. Most importantly, it was after midnight so I would only use up one travel day in case of connection later. That way I could save a little on a hostel for the night.

On a train, I received an SMS about entering new country few minutes before supposed arrival. Then, confused checked the GPS, it said we're near border, but train was stopping at a station like it was supposed to. Few minutes later, my phone updated timezone and I realized I've been tricked by train's timetable

Which means... We have one more hour today to find food and a place to sleep!

![](IMG_20240904_213644373.jpg)

After arriving in Porto, I've met fellow interrailers with whom we took one more train to main station, saving us both at least half an hour of walking. After leaving train station I heard Polish and said hi to apparently a fresh group of Erasmus students.

![](empty_phonebooth.jpg)

Then I went to cheapest hostel, tried to enter, someone let me in, went to reception to ask for prices (they were higher than hostelworld) and got kicked out because I had no reservation. Okay, wow.

![](porto_night.jpg)

So I ran to the other hostel to make it before 23, there I was confused looking for the door, which said reception to the left 50m so I went there and the door said reception to the right 50m. Thankfully, someone saw me and told me it's on the other side of the street, after entering, they were super friendly and let me book without problems.

![](are_you_a_cat.jpg)

So onto the dinner hunt: TGB salad at The Goodburger nearby. Sadly options at 11 pm are quite limited.

Day expenses: 37.09€
+10€ deposit for keycard
