Day in Valencia starts with... What a mistake last night calamari was... After asking around in bars around I've found one around the corner that allowed me to have hot water (which was upgraded to paid chamomile tea later but staff was really nice and actually helped me out, though Google Translate had to be used)

After converting additional EUR, ready to go! Went to Dulce de Leche Boutique near train station, where I've had a mini crossaint and a mini alfajor with a lemonade. There, I've chilled for 1.5h trying to figure out what to do: take a detour and go to Mallorca or continue my trip as planned and go towards home? 

![](dulce_de_leche.jpg)

![](alley.jpg)

Finally, after rambling some voice messages into the void, decided on continuing the journey and went to the train station to purchase seat reservations.

Made it few minutes before 21 when supermarket near hostel was supposed to be closing to purchase mineral water since locals gave me rather contradicting information regarding tap water safety.

Hunt for paella continues, I've seen earlier some relatively cheap one for 15€, but all I've found was one at Bar Cosmo for 13€, so the previous one probably closed. Then after eating and leaving I walk 20m and there's restaurant with Paellas. Cheaper with wider selection. And when walking hungry for 3h I've found none.
