>No sleep  
Train to train  
City to city  
No rest for the wicked

After sleeping for barely 1.5h, woke up half past 7am, packed and set off for breakfast in the city. During check-out, receptionist asked me what did I like the most about my stay in Amsterdam. 

I didn't quite compose myself together yet, after such short sleep and my answer was rather simple: "Not sleeping". Oh, so did I go somewhere and partied much? Not really, just hanged out with people. To which she replied that it's alright too.

Wait... so what exactly happened [yesterday](../day-6)? Oh right... I clearly haven't slept much. Hopefully I'll awaken on the way.

Before leaving, I went to the bar to fill my bottle. I've asked if I could by any chance perhaps get some lemonade in. Barkeep was still preparing today's batch, though they told me they could add some extra mint and water into my bottle. Fresh mint? Sounds great.

After leaving a hostel, there were some signs of night's rain, however the sky already cleared. On my way to the metro station, I had a company in a form of two Vietnamese people. Albeit we haven't talked much save for some short small talk, they were leading through the forest route. From my previous experience, it was a sub-optimal path. However, I was still a bit too wasted.

# Breakfast at Amsterdam

![Queue to the Omelegg||1000x500](queue.jpg)

Once I arrived near the central station, I went to the Omelegg for breakfast. After passing pancake restaurant I was considering on the way and finding the place, I was met with half an hour long queue for a relatively expensive-ish breakfast composed of an omelette in a crossaint with few add-ons. [Told ya](../day-5) I should lay off crossaint for my budgets sake...

After waiting in queue, having a family ahead of me, I was worrying I might not make it in time for train at 10 am and perhaps I should've gone to the pancake place instead. Once I got inside, people at the table next to mine were receiving their orders and I thought I might just take what they were having. Portions seemed quite comprehensive so perhaps it was worth the wait afterall. I had less around 30 minutes left and was still a bit worried I might be late for my train. Thankfully, the wait for food didn't take long, and in the meantime I've found that trains depart to the next city quite often.

Omelette was pretty good, should hopefully last me a while. Shortly after eating I've asked to pay and left for the train. It wasn't far from the train station either. Although, I may have ran here or there, or be curbed by traffic lights on a mostly empty road.

My first impression, of Amsterdam, right after I left the train, was that I didn't necessarly like the city. Lots of people, tourists, ticket gates on exit... I didn't like the city on my second day either: expensive, boring... However at the end, I found myself enjoying the city. All the people I've met during my stay, especially ones that made sleepless nights worth staying up for, and how friendly some people seemed to be... Or perhaps I just seem to cherish the experiences and stories I ended up having there.

# The Hague

At 10 am, I've boarded an hour long train to The Hague. 
On my way I've asked some friends whether I should stay the night in Rotterdam or go to Ant-werp. 

Everyone seemed to be voting for Rotterdam, though it seemed that it was mostly because they recognized the name more. I did also receive a list of recommendations to visit there.

![Lights at The Hague's train station|Center|1000x750](station_lights.jpg)

I've also got wind from a friend that our mutual friend lives in Rotterdam. I've messaged them, and after I've arrived in the Hague, I got response. So I walked around the train station and to some sort of a park nearby while texting for like half an hour. 

Sadly they weren't going to be in the city today so we wouldn't really meet, however another one lives closeby The Hague... whom I also didn't get to meet during my stay here.

![Running figuring holding beer||1000x650](beer_holder.jpg)

I've found some sort of a flea market nearby. I suppose it might be related to the last Sunday of the month. I've walked around a bit and then suddently, rain got wind of where I am and caught up with me once more. There seems to be no escape. Every city so far on a last day weather seems to be ~~crying~~ raining... 

Just as sudden as rain started, it stopped. I've ventured a bit further, and found myself near some sort of a lake, or a river. And would you know it? There was also some sort of flea market there too! On a second thought, not sure if it was the same kind though as this one also features Holland's cheese. With free tries. Even got to taste the cheese while being in Netherlands 😎 

As I walked, I've been thinking of perhaps grabbing something to eat, however nothing seemed to catch attention of my stomach. So I marched on, and passed few more Polish people. I've walked through some sort of main street with figures on pedestals. Right past a fast-food that does not accept cash and have it written on the doors. A stark contrast to Germany where it's the exact opposite in some places.

I've found myself at a bridge under which motorboat was flowed. I've been taking some pictures and people at one had to saw it because they've waved. Which I've only realized on one of the photos afterwards.

Then I've made my way back towards the train station. Before departing I've stopped at some sort of a shop-cafe nearby. I've had a thought of getting some sort of a souvenir or some choco-sticks, but then, journey barely began and I still have most of Europe to travel before going home.

On the train, I've compared prices of hostels and train tickets between Rotterdam, Ant-werp and Paris as I was considering skipping Ant-werp and going straight to Paris after staying in Rotterdam. It seemed to be slightly cheaper for me to go to Ant-werp for the night.

# Rotterdam

![Rotterdam's Train station|Top|1000x550](rotterdam_station.jpg)

At around 14 I've set off to the Rotterdam, train took a little over half an hour. 
After arriving I went once again hunt for food. There, I've browsed trip advisor for a while, and found Sugo Pizza nearby which caught my attention with pizza with Nutella. Strange combination, let's try it.

So I went there, and it turned out the pizza's were Roman style slices. Before going full psycho with chocolate I grabbed something more lunch-like: two slices, Chicken with pesto and arabiatta. It was alright if a little crispy.

While waiting for a pizza, I've asked Pizzaiolo, if by any chance he knows anything worth visiting around as I'm traveling and mostly just passing through today. He was a bit puzzled and told me that there's a lot of places to visit around Rotterdam, by train or by boat, though he'll think about things in the city and let me know in a bit. 

He then asked for input from his coworker and later once the pizza was ready he told me to visit Markthal, that it's a place that looks like a pen sharpener and that there's a tall building that looks like a pen next to it.

We chatted a bit more after I've ate my slices, and I've found out that he's originally from Portugal and came here for work as well as to live. He asked me if I'm going to Lisbon, and when I've confirmed, he gave me directions for [Pastel's de Belem](../day-19). Before going, I've also got slice with Nutella, since that's like the whole reason I came here in the first place, and, uhh... It was even more crispy. Not really my kind of pizza though. 

When I was leaving, he told that it was nice to meet and even wished me good travel.

![Park in Rotterdam||1000x700](park.jpg)

While walking towards the suggested place, Markthal, I've passed some stage with live music performers and a sort of picnic area which was located in front a church. I've walked in and found that the mass was about to start as I was there around 5 pm. Interesting timing for sure. There were even people giving printed out english translations of a sermon. 

The church had quite spacious interior, though what took me by surprise was lack of typically found wooden benches. In their place, half of available space was occupied by conference style chairs. They seemed to be mostly occupied too.

![Markthal building||1000x700](market.jpg)

As I ventured forward, some sort of square, or park next to current destination was under construction. When I was getting close, the rain started pouring. What a timing indeed. The building itself was surely big, though I have somewhat missed the tower nearby that was supposed to resemble pencil while trying to ran away from the rain (while surely taking photos). Other people closer to the building seemed to flock to the safety right way before I did, though there were some that didn't yet know what just started as they were leaving.

Markthal itself consisted mostly of small restaurants, bistros, cafes and candy stores. Since the ceiling was very high, some restaurants even had few umbrellas on top of them, possibly indicating terrace-like tables.

While trying to wait the rain out, I've navigated on my map to Erasmus Bridge as I still had over an hour until train. I wasn't sure how long the rain is gonna take so I just took my leave and attempted to leave however it was still pouring, so retraced back to the building and took some more photos. Few minutes later rain stopped and sky began to clear. While the world still looked quite wet I walked on. When I got closer to the river, streets were already dry.

I was walking, I've heard Polish-speaking people once more that seemed to be living there. Right, I'm in Rotterdam, Netherlands, I shouldn't be surprised. I've walked through some sort of Alley of Country Flags near the river, and then reached a miniature of a bridge.

![Closeup at the miniature bridge||1000x400](bridge.jpg)

There was even a telescope pointing straight at that miniature.

I've walked bit more and under a War Memorial monument few people were taking photos. Nearby there was Erasmus Bridge as well which seemed relatively low above water. On the other side of the river there seemed to be cruise ship that was way taller than the bridge.

Most tall buildings in the city also seemed to have non-standard architecture, each either challenging physics or breaking flatness of their walls in different ways.

I started walking back, on my way, near marina I've overheard more of Polish language. 
When I was crossing the street, on the crosswalk there was a black cable of sorts lying around. As I walked some more, there was another cable, straight up pointing out of the ground. Rotterdam, where cables grow out of the brick pavement. There were even red lights shining from beneach. Rotterdam, where electrical monsters seemingly live under the pavement.

Soon after, I was already nearby familiar shopping alley I passed on my way to the pizza earlier. So I thought about popping back once more, and did so. Portugeese pizzailolo was still working there so I thanked him for direction, confirmed it looked like he described, although I've missed the pencil and was on my way once more.

Close to the station, there was a car on second floor halfway outside the building. Interesting parking method for sure. I've heard music coming from one alley. So I took a little detour and walked straight into some small shady alleyway with grafitti all over the place. 

On the other side, on one of the walls there was a poster that said that there's a festival today. That explains the music. Though the current one wasn't really up my alley.

> Post trip note after investigating poster further: Not really my... Wait, there was Koven there?

I think I've passed like 8 groups of Polish speaking people on my short trip around Rotterdam.

I arrived at the train station shortly after from the side with still half an hour until train departure. So I walked around the place a bit, and they even had a miniature inflatable Eiffle tower on the second floor.

While waiting on the train, I've witnessed a pigeon just casually sitting in the middle of the platform like he doesn't give a duck. Staff was puzzled. They've asked the pigeon if they are alright and at first tried to reason with no-ducks-giving bird. Petractions have done nothing. Threats did nothing. Animal simply didn't care about anything in the world. Only after staff attempted to touch, they finally took a hint and flew away.

# Travel
Train arrived, all is good. Onward to Antwerp! On the train, I've rechecked hostels in Ant-werp and the one I was considering was now more expensive by around 30%. It was no longer more affordable than staying in Rotterdam. ...Should I leave the train before it departs? Too late now.

I was a bit sleepy so I attempted to take a nap (or rather, fall unconcious) which might have worked if not for the fact that... 15km later we stopped. I was confused and a bit alert trying to make sense of the situation, which possibly prompted my co passenger to say that the train broke down. Oh great. Another sign that I should've stayed in Rotterdam for the night? 

An hour later, we finally departed. Though I possibly may have stole a nap in the meantime. 

I was also running low on power in my phone and power socket wasn't working either. Uhh... a little bit of panic mode engaged. Thankfully, a fellow passenger allowed me to use their powerbank. Sadly, it didn't quite worked and I got maybe less than 5% tops (while the powerbank kept disconnecting). 

# Ant-werp

Or so I thought. When I've arrived in the city, my charge was barely at 30% level. though the train station seemed really big and spacious. What? Gotta enjoy the scenery anyway!

After leaving building, I pulled maps on my phone and I've realized another issue, a grave mistake indeed: I had not downloaded map for this city! I must've missed this city somehow back at home. 

![Building at night in Ant-werp||1000x700](antwerp.jpg)

I've checked the hostel again and... It was no longer available.
I found my way towards the hotel for which there seemed to be 4€ promo on the Trip Advisor, it might have been a scam but it perhaps it's worth a shot. Recepcionist confirmed it's a scam and they already had someone once who unfortunetly also *actually* paid for it. 

Uhh... That's a little bit rough. No map, no accommodation, night seems to be going to be chilly, and phone charge... is decreasing. Panic mode re-engaged (if it even disengaged).

What I did was walk resigned toward the train station and there I've heard English. Good good good. So I interjected their conversation by casually asking if they are visting the city by any chance and whether they have or know of any accomodation for the night.

> In the heat of the moment I didn't think of how it might seem, however In retrospect I  realize how such encounter might have seemed. 
> In my defense however, my appearance was probably bit wasted and worried sheepless with a backpack. Hopefully not threating despite strange choice of words I used.

They said they actually live there. I suppose I was expecting bit more Belgian from locals.
I've explained them my situation, that the hostel I tried to get wasn't available anymore, my phone was running low on power and I didn't have a map of the city downloaded and I have limited roaming.

One of them had a powerback that they allowed me to charge from while we talked, however, it wasn't quite working and kept shutting itself off. Most likely related to USB-C cable. They've also checked maps for hostels around and the only one they found was the one that was unavailable anymore on Hostelworlds. 

We chatted a bit and we were talking about something further away to which I've responded that I haven't quite figured city's communication system, which they thought I was talking about language and replied that Flanders in Belgium in general have a very high amount of English speaking people so that shouldn't be a problem. (Which also tracks with my preliminatry research earlier.)

I've chosen poor set of words for this, and clarified I meant transportation system. (Yay to using "city communication" for city transport in common speech in Polish)
Although, as they didn't have much to say about, probably not too complicated.

> I've asked about transportation because of my [previous experience](../day-5#tickets) in Amsterdam. Better be prepared in case they have some tips.

I've also asked if they recommend something in the city or how long I should stay there. They've mentioned that there's a lot of things around city, so similiarly to recommendation in Rotterdam.

I've also asked how safe the city is, and these girls told me that it's quite safe.

They also gave me a general direction of a hostel that may be open, so I thanked them for help and was on my way in the direction they pointed me towards.

On my way, I've stopped by in one restaurant to ask if they could maybe refill my bottle by any chance. Although, they were about to close they did agree. Sadly, they also threw out mint I had from Amsterdam, so I only had water now. Ouch.

I was quite thirsty so 600ml refill didn't last very long. I had to make a stop again. It was past 10 pm and place I've stopped by in was already closing, and staff told me they are closed. I've asked for a refill and after short hesitation they've agreed. Alright, hopefully that'll last me throughout the night.

I've reached out to my friends, or rather, I went ahead and ~~cried out~~ sent messages about my status. Then friend that would join me in Barcelona messaged me - *truly on-point to my present situation /s* - about Revolut and cashless payments. 

Walk to an unknown destination, in a foreign city with no map, glued to a phone while texting? Not ideal. It was also starting to be a bit chilly. The thing that tipped the scales was his message that he's starting to panic about the trip. Bro, I don't even have a place to sleep tonight, and you are panicking? I mean ok, fair point, it does not bode well for what may come next, but come on, Adventure!

I called him on a voice chat to hopefully resolve both his panic and my current predicament. My hostelworlds app wasn't loading for me however during our chat he checked hostels nearby and sent me links to some nearby. 

During our call, I've attempted to take some photos, although camera app seemed to hijack my microphone and friend couldn't hear me in those moments. 

Hostels from links didn't seem to show for me in app, however when I've reached an Old Town's square and the place where the hostel was supposed to be, everything seemed closed and dark. Ah well...

My battery was running low so I've entered bar Rococo that seemed still open. Really, first place nearby. I told my friend I'll go in and ask to recharge my phone so I may not hear him. At first, I intended on keeping the call ongoing so they could listen to how that chat goes, perhaps it would ease them a little and I would go back to the conversation right after. 

I've asked barman if I could charge my phone by any chance. He showed me the electrical socket near the floor. 

Well, the music was loud so I could barely hear them in my headphones. I've also realized one more thing: I can not have charge my phone and have headphones plugged in at the same time: they both use USB-C! So I disconnected from the call.

Electric outlet was in a terrible place as well, right near the door for the staff so if I would linger there any longer, I would annoy them. I've left my phone on the windowsill and took a seat at an empty table nearby so I could keep an eye on it.

After almost an hour, I had a full charge. I felt like I could also use a toilet, however I didn't want to seem rude so I asked for a drink. It was mostly just an excuse to ask for toilet really. 
However, I haven't spoken in an hour, and I felt like my voice might be not heard so I typed my order on phone and showed it to the barman. 

Message was really simple: "[...] I don't trust my own voice right now. [...] How much for something non alcoholic, without ice yet refreshing like a lemonade?"

> Why ask for price? Well, hostel was expensive af. I didn't want to accidently shoot myself off too. It was 5€. Managable. 

Barman asked me not once, but twice "no alcohol?" while preparing. Huh. Okay. At one point however, he took ice. ISo 've approached him to say that I asked for no ice either. 
I must've triggered some trigger boundry because he asked me to not go forward as it's staff only area. Uhh... Sure, just don't add ice. He realized what I meant and stopped.

It was a... bit too sweet drink for my taste though. When returning drink he asked if it the drink was good. Eeh, it was okay. I've also asked for direction to the toilet and afterwards I left.

![Old town in Ant-werp at night|Center|1000x600](antwerp_oldtown.jpg)

It was around 0:45 or so. Right after leaving the bar, I saw a similiar in age to me guy with a backpack. Wait a second. Fellow backpacker? I've approached him and asked if he stays anywhere by any chance.

There, he told me he doesn't go to hostels or hotels and instead sleeps on the streets and been traveling like that for years. Darn. What an encounter, especially now!

He told me he's from USA and only books flights to cities and money he saves on accomodations he spend on more flights more often.

He shared his insight from his experiences too. For choosing a place to sleep, he picks quiet backstreets, sometimes some back alleys, with benches and no people. Sometimes restaurant gardens, as fences around provide cover from wind. He then took a look around, and said places like these around are a little too busy so he wouldn't sleep there. He also told me that he even slept on benches in churches at night.

When it comes to sleeping, he tells me that feet are the worst so he takes of his shoes and changes socks or just let's his feet breath whenever possible, as well as socks/boots and carries a bunch for change. He then ties his backpack to his belt and goes to sleep.
He also mentioned that he had a case once when someone actually tried to take his backpack, however since it was tied to his belt, he woke up.

For food, he scavenges. Sometimes he grabs leftovers from uncollected plates in restaurants, and that it can be surprising how much people leave behind. And sometimes he does dumpster diving. I felt like I was reading some travelling blog when talking with him as some of things he was saying matched with what I read before. 

Whenever he needs water he asks for tap water staff at restaurants. Heh, something I did not that long earlier just as well.

And if he needs a toilet... he noticed some crates in the pavement and mentioned it as a last resort at night if he needs to relieve himself.

He also shared with me one trick he figured out while travelling when it comes to shower. That there are usually red light districts in cities, and he saw one here not far away as well.
He goes there and asks how much it would cost for just taking a shower, and nothing more. From what he tells me, they tend to be surprised, however they usually charge him around 5$ so he manages.

He told me that he used to buy souvenirs for his sister and family, however, he felt like they don't appreciate them so he stopped doing so. Though he still gets something from time to time.

He also carries a camera on which he likes to take photos. Other than that he has an iPhone and that's all he really needs as he has maps and can take photos with it as well. He tries to go to landmarks in cities he visits, usually at night as it's much quiter, takes photos whenever he can and then moves on. Sometimes he takes a bus or a train to the next city, sometimes he flies back home.

> Meeting such traveller, especially in that moment felt quite like a surreal experience. What are the chances?

Truth be told, encounter with him kind of reassured me that perhaps tonight will be fine. I also thought eh, what the hack, I'll need that map of the city probably. I decided to sacrifice some of my roaming data for the trip and download it anyway. It would mean I might need to be more frugal about my data usage later but currently? Priorities - survive.

I didn't feel like trying to find a peaceful bench to sleep as the night was getting colder, so I went toward the train station hoping that maybe I could last the night there.

![Antwerp's train station at night||1000x550](night.jpg)

On my way, I've stopped to take some photos of a central station from the distance. And so did 3 other people soon after I started taking photos. With one of them, we even briefly and silently compared our takes. It was a rather sudden and unexpected interaction around 1:40 am.

I've reached the train station afterwards, and I think I've passed on my way few homeless people and probably a dealer. Uh... Guy was sitting on a step, was in the process of rolling something that looked like a joint, and said to someone, probably homeless(?), that he only spreken in Netherland.

However, when I tried to enter the station I found that it's closed shut. Oh-oh. That's... not too good.... At. All.

There was an underground garage and it was bit windy, so finding a place to shelter from wind could be good enough.

When I found an way inside, on the way, I've passed people covered in blankets sleeping - homeless. As I ventured further down, what hit me immedietaly was a smell. Oof, some people must've used this place as their toilet.

![Bike park||1000x500](bike_parking.jpg)

There was a bicycle garage and further down, closed, car park. That's not going to cut it. I'mma take my chances elsewhere. I've headed out.

It's around 2 am. and night gets colder and colder, while I only have shorts and no accomodation whatsoever. 

I took a stroll around city until some time later, I've heard some cool music coming from one place. It seemed like some bar. I've stood there for a while, listening to music and considering if I should enter, but then, I would only sit there so could I even? What if they have some private party?

Moment later, someone came out and asked if I'm ok?

> Am I ok? Duudee...

I've mentioned that my train was late and all hostels were already closed so I don't really have where to spend the night.
He sympatized with me, said that they are actually closing up already however there's a cafe nearby that's open until 6 am. Though, they tend to be busy and I might not be welcomed there however it might be worth a try.

So I walked some more in the direction I was given and shortly after, I've reached place that was mentioned. It was indeed bustling with live so late at night in front of it on this day. 

I've strolled around a city a bit further, and then some time later I've circled back.

Then, I entered this Cafe Bombai. There was a table open and once I took my seat I was immedietaly approached by the staff asking if I want to order something. I asked if it would be fine if I just sat there for a while. The answer was prompt and simple: No.

Oh, okay. I'll take my leave. So only thing left for me was to continue walking I suppose.
Not far away I've found an open... bar, restaurant, dinner of sorts? It was quite empty and barkeep seemed rather bored. Slow night, huh?

I've asked if it's fine if I could just sit for a while there, if it's okay. They just shrugged it off and said it's fine. I took a seat at one of the banquette booth and started summarizing day and picking photos to share for the day. 

A bit later, a few drunk people came in and sat nearby. They were arguing about something that if they would be sober they would realize how pointless their argument is before it would even begin. 

> I would like to say that I attracted crowd to this fine establishment that allowed me shelter from cold, by just sitting there and making crowd, however there were little chances someone actually spotted me from outside considering my seating was back to the entrance and thus windows.

A while later barkeep approached them to take their orders and also stopped by me to ask if I would like anything. Not really if it's okay. He shrugged again and went away, back to watching some sort of sports channel. 

At 3:20 am there seemed to be a little bit of a problem, I've started falling asleep. One of the guys that came, at the next table was already passed out. Meanwhile, his friend turned his attention to me and we started chatting. He told me his friend had some problems with his wife so they were here. I've also learned from our conversation that train station opens at 4 am. Good to know. I could try survive rest of the night there...

> Just in time to keep me awake.

When we were chatting, he asked me where I'm from. I've replied that I'm from Poland. He said I don't look Polish, as most Poles he saw had short hair. Meanwhile I... he was thinking for a while and then finally said that I look more like... Johnny Deep.

Huh, that's... interesting.

After our conversation, I considering my options. I've found a train that would take me to Lille and then to Paris at around 4:36 am. Literally one of the first trains out of here. Also it seemed like a cheaper option than others I was considering earlier.

When I was leaving, I believe the guy I was chatting with was also already passed out. 

I went to the train station, and side entrance was indeed already open and people were also going in.

At 4:30, train was already on a platform waiting and I could already enter. Considering I could get some sleep on the train, I decided duck it, let's go to Paris.

![Table on a train||1000x800](night_train.jpg)

Shortly after departure I grabbed remaining stroopwaffle out of my backpack in order to eat it. I know, not the best dinner, or food at all, especially considering my today's menu. But it was what I had left, and also something that was meant to be eaten earlier rather than later.

After taking a few bites, conductor came asking for tickets. So soon? Eh, perks of being one of the first passengers I suppose. So I put my waffle away, hopefully wiped any leftover carmel and pulled phone out, navigated to the rail planner app and before I shown my ticket, I navigated to enable travel day for today. Conductor was a bit sceptical at first and asked if I have a ticket to which I replied that I have interrail, I just decided to take the train at a last moment and didn't update my schedule yet. There, I've activated another travel day and showed the QR code. All good.

After ticket was checked, I went back to consuming what remained of stroopwaffle.
With beast inside satisfied, and barely any energy left, I've sent a message that I'm going to Paris to some other friends before 5 am. I've set up alarm few minutes before arrival, and then, the music started...
