I've been a hobo for 4 nights by now.

![](breclav.jpg)

With group of people from yesterday we've almost embarked on the night train to Berlin, however just in time the one to Poland seemed to arrive, so after finding it online I told people our train got here. So we left the train and went to the other one hopeful... Only to find out it has been cancelled and finished its run in the city we were waiting for it. 

![](possible_connections.jpg)

So we had like 3h we could chill on the train before it would go back to Munich. Thanks for the croissant I've received for my way to Prague, I had something to get me going.

Afterwards I've asked around people and accidentally, became sort of a local guide for the wagon full of lost people who even gave me a pretzel to keep me going further, and then orange scarf I've tied into my hair so they could see me if they had lost me.

[Few days ago](../day+25/index.html#Pizza) it might've seem like maybe I looked like some kind of a hobo at that point, however now, when I've spoken with people and told them some of my recent stories, and after actually being a hobo for a few days, people said that I don't actually look like one, crazy right?

While I was doing a recon, the group 
stumbled upon few people with car who agreed to take one older man from our group to Poland. Once I've arrived with resignation and bad news that no train goes to Poland at all, I've found out about their car as I was looking for alternatives with Flixbus. 

Once they learnt I only have one single backpack and remaining two people from our group said I could go they agreed for me to hitch a ride with them to Katowice. There, I've returned the scarf, we've bid our farewells and I'm on the way home
