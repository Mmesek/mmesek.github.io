Yet no rest for the wicked  
Fellow travellers  
Yet no respite from the rain

Second day in Amsterdam! 
Plan for today? A breakfast at the hostel, and off to the train after 11 am. Walking tour, stroopwaffle workshop and a museum of House music.

Stopped at a station near the local market a little before noon. There, I took a stroll around for a while. 

![River|Center|900x600](river.jpg)

However, I didn't rest well and felt incredibly sleepy. So I took a break at a bench on which I've almost fell asleep, which made me realize my error - only way to stay awake was to keep going. 

# Walking tour

![Walking tour|Bottom|x400](walking_tour.jpg)

As I walked a bit more, I stumbled upon a walking tour to which I've decided to just tag along with. No one said a thing. Inflitration successful. One of the things can be crossed off, yay!

After walking tour, I've wandered a bit more myself. Then I've set out to try make it for wafflemaking workshop at 2 pm.

![Anonymous with monitors||900x700](anonymous.jpg)

On my way, I stopped for a moment to check what's going on the square as there was a group of people with Anonymous masks and monitors. While I was trying to make sense of what's being displayed on monitors (which was a rather daunting task due to light and their chosen screen types), I was approached by one girl with mask on an arm. 

She then proceeded to explain that they were showing animal cruelty and began asking me how many more animals would have to die for me to go vegan. I've played a little devil's advocate and we reached a consensus that even vegans hurt animals when it comes to certain skin conditions or pharmaceuticals.

Conversation felt rather rigged and I wanted to reach my destination before 2 pm so I had to bail as I had like 10 minutes left. Short stop to figure out what Anonymous is doing here turned into a couple of minutes stop due to few vegan activists posing as Anonymous.

On my way, I've heard some more Polish people, which I intended on striking a short conversation with and maybe gather some info about places to go in Amsterdam but somehow my internal translator had a slight lag after encounter with activists. 

What happened? I've spurred a simple hi in Polish, which received a rather strange look which was a mixture of "they thought they heard something but weren't sure" and "their family was just murdered and house burned to the ground". So I just bailed this increasingly awkward situation by saying some random nonsense in Polish like "err.. I mean..."  which somehow has resolved the situation into the category "they heard something" instead of "someone just murdered their entire family including extended one, burnt their house down and danced on their grave". Phew. Close one.

It was too late for me to make it for 2 pm so I changed plans and went to the De Laatste Kruimel. Their muffin of a day was vegan. Eh, let's try it after having conversation on said topic no longer than an hour earlier. There, Happy? Although, that muffin is not this place's speciality is what I can tell you.

Truth be told, I wasn't really enjoying Amsterdam at the moment, not sure why exactly. A little bit boring? It's strange how it didn't strike me as expensive, even though in retrospect it's horrendously expensive. People maybe? I had a bit of mixed experience, although most "negative" ones were related to passerby strangers on the streets.

![Dancing Houses||900x700](dancing_houses.jpg)

From there, I went to Our House to ask for prices and spots left for later as I wasn't sure if what I found online was the same and in case I wouldn't make it for workshop. It wasn't fully booked out or anything and price was same so all good, I would come back later.

I took a stroll to the nearby bridge where some stranger was asking for directions to train station, however people didn't quite seem to know how to point him. Meanwhile I had map application currently open so I asked where exactly he needs to go. Although, having a map and plotting a route is one thing. Relaying this information on a street level in a simplified way and preferably in a single sentence... is another entirely. Just take this road and keep going left-right? Hopefully he arrivied at his destination.

After that I walked towards so called nearby tourist attraction I've spotted on the map called "Dancing Houses". Once I've reached the pin, the place didn't look anything out of ordinary, moment later I saw people taking some photos and knew that's the place.

# Stroopwaffle Workshop

Afterwards, I've proceeded towards the Rembrandt Square where the last wafflemaking workshop available for the day at 4 pm was supposed to be happenening. There was no reservation available anymore as I tried to book it too close to start of an event.

I didn't have much information to go on so I just circled around the square on which workshop was supposed to be happening. There wasn't many people at the moment so when after a moment a group of people seemed to gather around one in chef's hat, I approached them and asked if it was still possible to join. Chef told me it wasn't a problem as they should have a slot available, and that we can sort it out after, though he did explicitly mention I still would have to pay. No worries, though I'll admit that trying to get in for free being a possibility this way didn't cross my mind at first. I have only asked if the price would be the same to which chef said he doesn't know the price as back office handles that stuff, but he said it should be the same.

We walked into the nearby building which turned out to be one of biggest night clubs in the city. Although, chef also mentioned it's not the best one ;) Which kinda matches my experience at my home town.

During workshop we got aprons and ingredients like flour, dry yeast, cinnamon, sugar, powdered egg, salt, butter and water, which were already measured for us. Sadly, I don't have any photos from the processed as my hands were a bit sticky from dough. There was one food influencer that even had a tripod and friend dealing with camera though.

It was explained how we had to prepare the dough, which reminded me of how you make one for pizza - mix all ingredients and then knead, and once it becomes solid, beat it, throw into the bowl, throw between hands et cetera for few minutes. At the end we were supposed to have two equal-sized balls.

While preparing dough I was asked for organizational matters like email address to set my reservation up. I may need a simpler address at some point though...

After we had dough ready, it was time to bake it. For this, a waffle iron was used. The thing was already heated on both sides and had a built-in timer. We had to place one ball inside, close it for like 60s and after light turned green take it out. However... the light never switched for me and counter didn't start, so I asked around, and when Chef came and investigated, he asked 
"How long ago did you put it in?" 
"Uhh... maaybe 30 seconds ago by now?" 
"Ok, so let's just keep it in and just take it out."

Despite the hurdles, it went well. After taking it out there was a "pocket of air" inside so we were supposed to cut it from the side to open it up. After that, it was time to put caramel in. While chef said to be generous with it because extra will go out anyway, I feel like I went a little too far with amount of it anyway as it tasted... far too sweet afterwards.

Then, it was about merging both sides back together and it was possible to decorate it, add chocolate and the like.

Last step was to leave it to cool so they would properly bind. Chef also recommended eating half of the first one while it was still warm, so I took a few bites and... it was ducking amazing. However as I didn't quite eat anything aside of muffin, I didn't feel like eating it right away.

![Stroopwaffles||900x500](stroopwaffles_workshop.jpg)

Then we repeated the exactly same process for the second dough-ball and after being done we went to eat now cooled first ones. I'll admit, fresh hot one was far better, though the cool one was still pretty good. Just not as good ;)

We have also received certificates of completion the workshop, which was pretty nice. Too bad I only had a drawstring bag and no way to secure the paper from bending. The second stroopwaffles we packed as there were plastic bags available to store it.

While eating, I've received an email with payment however the price was higher than original, which prompted me to ask about it. Chef asked me about to wait a moment.

After that, back office girl came and was looking for me as she didn't know if I was still around. She couldn't apply the discount directly however it was possible that I would book for tomorrow and she would just move my reservation around to keep the price.

All in all, Organizera were so nice they've even allowed me to retain GetYourGuide discount. And waffles? Amazing. Though, I should've ate the first one right while it was still hot.

As I was leaving, I went to wash my hands. Considering toilet was occupied and there was a fellow workshopper waiting for someone as well, we chatted a while about what brought us to Amsterdam and travel in general.

# Our House

Workshop ended a little after 5 pm. I walked towards Our House, there, I've asked for availability again. Next slot would be past 6 pm and I still had a bit over 30 minutes. So I went further towards the bridge to maybe take a stroll or some more photos in the meantime. However as I reached the bridge, the rain started falling. I went back to the museum to wait it out at the doors. 

Rain however, started pouring more vigorously and there was very little hope of it stopping. Even passerby's came to shelter there as well. I decided to buy the ticket and went to wait in the souvenir shop part of the museum which double acted as a waiting room

I could use also use a toilet so I went to look for one. The doors were poorly labeled so it wasn't clear which one is which. However, there was some issue with male one: The paper (any paper) was missing. There also wasn't any dryer. As I was already wet from the rain, I couldn't just brush it off my shirt either. There was someone leaving female one and I've asked if they had any there. There was. Phew. No one around, let's try. 

While I was trespassing, some guy opened the door and asked if it's the male one. The doors were on my side. However, let's not abuse that. I've told him the paper was missing in our's hence why I'm here, as I was leaving, I've shown him it's the next door. 

!["Here's to the nights that turned into mornings, and the friends that turned into family" at Our House||1100x500](quote.jpg)

Soon after, organizators came and shown us entrance. They collected tokens that served as a tickets while we were entering.

The first area was museum-like with Deadmau5 head exhibited behind a glass. There were other heads spread around the museum too. Grotesque museum-like.

Next area was a like bar in a club. With actually functioning bar and a few people ordered drinks there as well. There was an introductory movie sequence displayed about the museum itself.

Then, they've opened third, main area with a dancefloor. Interactive exhibits like DJ console or sequencers were in adjusting corridors around the dancefloor. There were DJ consoles with relatively simple game on beats and danceability meter, a photo booth with wearable heads of DJs, table-sized big sequencer, wall-sized sequencer showcasing inner workings, and a bunch of regular sequencers as exhibits.

Someone asked me to take a photo of them with one of the heads so I could [be a photographer](../day-2#chocoversum) once more. 

There were two people at the table-sized sequencer. We started chatting and turned out one of them is a DJ! After a while, we were summoned back to the dancefloor.

While waiting on the dancefloor for everyone to gather, one man with earbud headphones (which I found peculiar in such place, although I didn't ask about), started chatting with me. A bit of small talk in the meantime. 

There on the walls around was displayed a tad bit far too long movie-sequence with fog machine near the end. Movie was the last sequence of the museum, there were still few minutes to take some photos at the initial area as we were making our way to the exit. 

Afterwards I've chatted with some people in the lobby, one group were visting their friend there and as I found out they were also staying at the same hostel as I did! I've asked if they are planning on going to the party at the hostel later and they were planning indeed!

I wasn't sure myself as I wanted to explore more of a city but maybe?

Anyway, I've chatted with few more people and met some locals, I've asked if they know about any worthwhile places or things to do and aside of an answer like "Here? In Amsterdam? Hah, good one! There isn't even a Dutch cuisine, but there's a ton of italian" (wait, what did I eat yesterday then?!) I got few recommendations in northern side of the city.

# Fellow Travelers

It was around past 7:30 pm and I didn't have much plans left so I considered getting something to eat. As I was walking I've been recording a few voice messages while making plans for next few hours. The idea was to go to the vegan place I've had a pin from day earlier to eat something. Then to go through Red Lantern district as *many* friends seemed *very* insisting on me going there. And perhaps even check that place in northern part I got recommendation for, or at least just walk there, and then head to the hostel and check out the party there.

While passing through on my way to the area where I ate day earlier, there was a lot of rather unsigned black-colored vans... Makes one wonder what they transport... Of course with kind regards for any undercover police ;)

I've reached the place from my pin past 8 pm, although nothing really caught my interest there. I was thinking of maybe going to one pizzera. Though, I didn't feel like ordering a pizza by myself so I passed on that.

I've also checked some of earlier recommendations, although there didn't seem to be anything going on today that would interest me enough to go.

I've decided to go towards the district and head to the hostel. Around 8:30 pm when I was in familiar alleyways from yesterday, what or rather who I've found was a group of interrail travellers from Poland! 

I've heard three Polish travellers window shopping some dresses. This time around, I came prepared: "How long are you having fun staying in Amsterdam?" (which in Polish sounds a ton better, trust me "Jak długo zabawiacie w Amsterdamie?")

It caught them a little bit by surprise as one of them started to answer in English. When I asked if they are interrailing by any chance - they were indeed! 

> How to spot an interrailers 101...

They told me know each other from some Find a travel buddy group on Facebook and that only part of their group won interrail while others flew here from Poland. In France, they apparently didn't have anywhere to stay and someone from couchsurfing-like alternative website took them in where they've slept in some unsigned van. That's some rather... daring decision right there. One of them told me she actually took a look at driver seat and it was filled with stuff so she wasn't too afraid they would wake up somewhere else. Respect for forward thinking and courage in such case! 

They've been to Barcelona and apparently one of them even lost an ID there, though they somehow managed to stay off the grid even when booking to a hostel, she didn't seem particullary worried either as their trip was coming to an end. They also told me about TooGoodToGo being a good option for cheap food and that it's quite popular in the west. I've stumbled on it before but didn't think of trying yet.

When we walked into the nearby square, we witness bunch of birds playing King of the Hill with some sort of a trash. Which bird will try to take it? Soon after the game we stopped by for a street perfomancer that was juggling with fire. In rain. With wind.

We walked a while, and were thinking about getting something to eat. They've mentoned they were in some countries like France and tried crepes in Spain or something along these lines. And they didn't try churros in Spain or Belgian Fries in Belgium. As I personally haven't eat churros before either, I thought of trying now so I've found some place nearby with churros.

As we were walking, we passed one place with Belgian Fries which one of them really wanted. So we took a detour, and waited in queue in rain. Standing there I was feeling how one of my shoes is strangely soaked. Not good.

After that we went to the Churro's place, got Churros and even ordered Dutch mini pancakes. There, a little bit of Dutch cuisine too ;) Their 4th friend came from the hostel and joined us in the restaurant.

They had Revolut so splitting the bill was relatively easy - They just had to scan my QR code and type value to send. There was however a small hitch after we got the check: It was a bit more than I initially thought as I didn't account for nutella. All good, they were fine with sending the extra, although Revolut's limitation came to light: You can't send less than 1€ to someone. So we approached it in a way that one of them would send me remaining sum +1€, then I sent 1€ back. There, all is hopefully balanced.

![House of Cards||900x600](house_of_cards.jpg)

Afterwards we set out to explore the pub side of the night city. We walked into one, and they ordered shots. As I'm the abstinent, I was just serving as a sober mascot as usual. ~~I'm starting to doubt there are any people that don't drink alcohol left.~~ There was a slot machine in the place we were and some of the girls mentioned they were thinking of doing some gambling on the trip. The machine however, was currently occupied.

So in the meantime, one of them started building house of cards while another was sketching. Few minutes later and machine was still occupied, so we took our leave.
Next place we went to was a bar next to some club near Rembrandt's Square. We sat under umbrellas in front of it while rain was still lightly pouring outside.

After a moment, we found out QR code on a table used to order things. It redirected to a website, however there were no query parameters in URL address which made me suspicious of it. Turned out QR code had a redirection which set up a cookie in the process. Neat, no messing around with table numbers. After a moment, waiter came with drinks.

We sat out there for a while, though the benches we were sitting on weren't exactly dry, and there was a loud party at a nearby table. We went to place where they were day earlier which was more chill, was dry and allowed better options for chatting. Despite it being a sports bar.

When they've found I have mini light ring on my phone around camera *(Nothing A1)*, they've asked for me to take a few photos. And that way, I could've roleplay as a photographer once more. I've connected to the wi-fi hotspot in the bar and uploaded images to a shared folder on a cloud and sent them links.

Their friend who was drawing, sketched me as well. She said she wasn't particularly fond of it, though I was still impressed by what she managed to do. ~~Of course, that also meant if anything would go sideways police wouldn't even need to get any Forensic Artist to sketch me from their descriptions.~~

![City street at night||1000x400](night.jpg)

2 am was closing in on us, and they had a flight at 6 or 7 am so it was a moment to call it a night. We went towards where they were staying and one of them walking surpringly fast to which she replied that it's how she normally walks.

Nightlife around 2 am seemed quite lively on some streets we were passing through, compared to my experience day earlier around midnight. We even passed near Red Latern District on our way where they told they were there day earlier as well.

We reached the hostel... or actually, a boat as it turned out around 2:20 am. it was raining outside and I took a shelter for a moment to hopefully wait it out.

One of them went straight packing and to sleep while other one took shower. 
Rest of them have shown me how tiny their rooms were, or rather, that their rooms were basically berths hidden in a wardrobes with very little room outside that. 

As I thought they were waiting for their friend to finish occupying bathroom we chatted a bit more. At one point I've realized I'm actually keeping them from preparing for leaving. So I've bid them one final farewell and set out on my way. Great many thanks for letting me tag along ;)

![Calm before the storm at the pier||1000x500](night_river.jpg)

# Way Back

It was already heavy raining at 3 am and I had a bus in... 5 minutes?! Holy sh.. Nothing like night run during rain. I also had to find the correct bus stop and attempting to do so in bus route app while raining and as I was soaking wet didn't make it any easier.

I've crossed a street and stopped at the wrong bus stop and on the wrong side of the street, so I had to cross it again. There was a bus and I didn't know if it's the right one. It was raining and I couldn't make sense of the numbers. As I entered, I've asked if it goes to Diemen but apparently was a bit out of breath and wasn't speaking clearly enough so I didn't get the answer. I took a chance and jumped on the bus to figure it out on the way, better than to stay and soak in this heavy rain.

It was the correct bus and 26 minutes later I've arrived. Although having a mixed experience with night buses before and as I didn't recognize surrounding streets, I asked people on the bus if it's the correct stops. Someone told me once we reached the correct station that's the one. 

Turned out, this bus route didn't go as near the hostel as yesterday's one so I had to walk for another 20 minutes or so. At least it wasn't raining so heavly where I was at the moment. It might not have been raining, however, the sky was alight as it would be on a day due to thunderstorm on the horizon.

I've arrived at the hostel around 3:48 am. There was party evening/night happening earlier so I went to the bar area in case there was still someone. And there were still couple people left! They seemed to be chatting, and drinking lemonade. I didn't drink in a while so I could use a drink too. I asked about it and one guy showed me there's a jar with dispenser. Nice, I took a glass and joined the conversation by asking about the party. Turned out it wasn't anything special as there were some kids from some reason.

After a moment one guy called it a night and only one stayed. Remaining one told me that he only recently made a passport as he was from USA of Russian origins and went to Europe to visit it by the flixbus. Our conversation turned to games for a moment as he was also a fan of MOBAs, and as Deadlock was new I asked it. We had a bit of conversation about it and when I suggested I could drop him an invite to beta he said that he had some issues with addicition and didn't want to go back to that. Fair stance, also congrats on beating bad habits ;)

We chatted almost until 5 am. I had probably around 2h of sleep ahead of me. Speaking about beating bad habits...
