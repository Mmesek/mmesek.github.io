TooGoodToGo from BackWerk available at Hbf, hmm... Let's do this. It took a while to find it, and there I've met another person who also came for TGTG. We both arrived when pickup was about to start and... Cashier had no clue. Turned out, it was at a different BackWerk, on the other side of the rail station.
