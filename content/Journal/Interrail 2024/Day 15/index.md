Before leaving Barcelona I thought about eating breakfast at the hostel's bar, but barkeep mentioned they aren't allowed to have eggs, so I was a bit worried about their scrambled egg's quality. Instead, I went to The Egg Lab and got Smokey York Eggs Benedict. Sadly that means I won't try churros place near Sagrada that was recommended to me. Perhaps in Madrid...

![Smokey York Eggs Benedict at The Egg Lab](breakfast.jpg)

Waiting for food and then issues with terminal took so long, that I had to run back to hostel, stormed into the room, took my backpack from the locker, wished good day to roommates and while leaving one of them was probably half shocked holding his hand to wave. In and out, clear extraction

I was supposed to be 30m before departure at the train station, managed to get there at 10:34 despite leaving hostel at around 10

After arriving at the train station I was greeted with luggage check. Apparently that's Spain for you

After arriving in Madrid, we decided to get tickets to Porto sorted out first thing. After waiting for over 2h(!) for our turn at ticket office, we only managed to book seats for Madrid-Pontevedra, as machine refused to work for lady serving us after this. Apparently that's also Spain for you as they don't have self-service machines
![Garden at Madrid Train station](madrid_garden.jpg)

While we were waiting there were other interrails who were late on their train and needed to exchange tickets, yikes

![Building in Madrid](madrid_city.jpg)

![](IMG_20240902_173131399.jpg)

From there we tried to use the metro, but it required extra fee for issuing a card, so we decided to walk to the hostel. After refreshing, we embarked onto walking around the city, where we went for pupusas from TooGoodToGo at a market nearby

![Pupusas with friends](pupusas.jpg)

Then we went to Decathlon as my boots... Have seen everything by now. Though, lack of my size left me only getting a towel

RFID at checkout

Friends wanted to had dinner at Taco Bell and then when we saw Dunkin' at TooGoodToGo we decided to take a package as well

![Donuts from TooGoodToGo](donuts.jpg)

![](IMG_20240902_223330170.jpg)

![](IMG_20240902_224420679.jpg)

At the end, we wanted to check terrace bar at the hostel, but it turned out to be closed on Mondays :(
