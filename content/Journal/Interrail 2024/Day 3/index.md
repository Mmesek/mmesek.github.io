>Wasted in the morning  
Cloudy day  
Crossed out plans  
Rainbow still shines through

Bit less than 4h of sleep, and the fact that I was beat after yesterday didn't quite help me with waking up. Eh, what a great way to start third day...

After I somehow brought myself to a usable state, I was greeted with homemade croissants my host has made. Delicious! Okay, they brighten up the day a little. Now we are ready to roll. Or to attempt some balancing on a tightrope, courtesy of my host.

Managed to go to the center around 11am.
After light breakfast made of aforementioned croissants, the plan was to go to the cafe with free cake for having Hamburg Card. Already bought it yesterday and the return of investment seemed rather pale, so why not... Well, I would have if rain didn't catch me in the middle, which I had to wait out at a metro station... Or if said cafe wouldn't be closed... Off to a great start!

![Sign saying that owner is on a vacation between 19th and 27th||700x400|](closed.jpg)

While figuring out what to do next, rain stopped, clouds moved away and I had a new destination: German Customs Office!

After taking another train... Well... Change of plans, again: According to Hostelworld there's a meetup event happening at a hostel near main train station. That'll probably happen later, but I could check the lay of the land and ask around what's it about. I just need to go back one station to take different train. Not really a problem.

After finding the place and letting receptionist finish her call, upon asking about the event from hostelworld... She had no clue about it, though told me to check at the bar as these events usually happen there. But it's closed currently and opens after 5pm, well... Going to check again later in that case. Great recon.

![Saint Michael's Church|Top|](church.jpg)

Fine, let's visit St. Michael's Church! It's a stationary object so what could go wrong, right? Almost, there was a service which meant it was closed! Thankfully, I've arrived couple minutes after it ended 😎 "Free" sections involves main area and a demo version of a museum in catacombs. Rest was sadly paid.

What happened next? Next it started raining... Again ;) And I was already out in the nearby park trying to figure out where to go next. Not many places of shelter from the rain. A little roof extend near underground entrance was not nearly enough so I took a stroll in rain after managing to wipe my phone's screen just enough to check the map (and take far too many photos while doing so. It's 3rd day and I'm already taking too many photos)

Thankfully, it stopped after a short while, so I made my way to the harbor. While going there, there was a spot where I've trained balancing a little as morning session taught me thing or two I could practice. 

After walking down the small section of harbor, took a stroll around, and went up the stairs, on which... Cops are running right in my direction! Oh come on, what did I do wrong this time? I'm pretty sure tracks were covered and rain washed any traces of me...
They pass me. Phew, Ok, good, they didn't know, phew. 

Afterwards, I accidentally passed through Red Lantern district, (or was in some kind of a strange main street? Hard to tell), and a police station right next to it, at least they planned were to make an HQ, I guess. Considering proximity, no wonder police were on foot.

![Gargoyle at the docks||400x400](gargoyle.jpg "Ivy, is that you?")

Circling back, and walking few inner streets where gust of wind was strong enough to force me to put on jacket again, went back through the harbor aaand... You guessed it! Rain started once again. From there, took train to a restaurant called Underdocks, (despite the fact that there was one at the harbor), where as a weary traveler managed to find a shelter where waitress allowed me to use their powerbank, so big thanks for that! Food was also brilliant. And I'm not saying it just because by then I was already a little *too* hungry.

![Hamburg's Panorama|Top|900x500](panorama.jpg)

Next climbed Stadtgarten as it was nearby, to get a view of Hamburg's panorama and took a bus straight to German Customs Museum.... After 5pm, so after they closed it. Ah... Perhaps I could've tipped after all...

On my way to Diehalle, I've stumbled upon a pro that just moved here from LA and was looking for an entrance just like I did. That was a rather unexpected encounter. His surprise that staff didn't know who he was when he mentioned his name or team was a bit amusing. Curse of modern fame in a world that fights for scraps of attention in daily life, yet still constrained by the veil of local bubbles, I suppose?

After a little chat, and perhaps just a tiny bit of borderline-accidental social engineering, I trained there for a while; messed up some flip attempts to sponges to reach a psychological paralysis level, and decided to move on, try some rolls and from some reason reverse rolls too, and a short flow. During which I managed to test my camera paracord etui in action. Sadly result is too... variadic? to use it reliably.

![Rainbow on the cloudy background|Top|](rainbow.jpg)

While going to check event at the hostel again, there was a light rain and a rainbow shined through! 
After reaching hostel, found out at the bar that they moved event to Friday a *long* while ago... yay...

![Sunset over the city||800x400](sunset.jpg)

Exhausted, mostly from taking photos and not eating rather than training (trust), I went on a hunt for dinner, which was found in Max & Consorten in a form of their Salad, which was... Alright. Yeeeaaah... I was running out of options and daylight as it was already past 9pm.

Made my way back to the host's place, chatted a bit, and as I was going to catch a train in the middle of the night, we bid ourselves farewells. Thanks again for letting me stay ;)

> Day cost: 23.9€
