On the way back home, but who said I have to go straight home? I have to cross entire country anyway, which leaves me with a couple of choices to spend most of the day at, like for example at Wrocław!

![](wroclaw_gnome.jpg)

Few years ago, friend told me about a plan to go around Europe. We would go this or next year. Back then I've only been once to Wrocław do I've asked if we can finish the trip as one of final destinations there. While that friend said no word about said plan ever since, I've executed it over a month with a break. Guess where I'm finishing?

![](wroclaw_fountains.jpg)

It also seems to be the city I've been visiting more often lately, and from some reason, it's a third year in a row to happen in October, so why not, let's do it again!

![](wroclaw_street.jpg)

Of course, I'm already familiar with city layout from my previous trips. So just walking around is not going to cut it. Alright, let's try some breakfast! 

![](wroclaw_church.jpg)
Turkish Eggs, with dice roll smoothie recommendation. I truly need to stop asking staff for recommendations. Or internet for that matter.

![](puro_hotel_sign.jpg)

Stroll down the memory lane of my first visit to Wrocław in reverse, 

![](wroclaw_river.jpg)

![](wroclaw_trip.jpg)

and I'm on board train going to Poznań. Just because.

![](poznan_grafitti.jpg)

In Poznań I go to old town on auto pilot, 

![](poznan_old_town.jpg)

go for quesadillas made from pancakes and then go for the train station, earlier than planned. 

![](poznan_park.jpg)

As I walk to the platform, someone asks if I could spare a moment, to which I promptly answer with question if English would be possible. 

![](poznan_train_station.jpg)

Such fear is a rare sight, and a strange one to witness among young people. Still have roughly 15m until train departure and seems like they struggle to get anyone to stop by. So I step in, speaking Polish this time, and we chat for a while, and would you know it? This tactic has worked, it attracted attention from someone else! Their boss.

I thought petition scam is like a baseless form of scam, but turns out it's based on paper-based automatic subscription sign up, a subscription they were trying to get people to subscribe to.
